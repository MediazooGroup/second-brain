# Part 3 - hand the tidying over

**Wed 12 Aug, 1pm BST. One hour.**

## The promise

Watch an assistant file a mess into a clean note, then keep a living wiki
current, with you getting the last word every time.

## Why this is the one the course builds to

Every note system you have abandoned died the same way. It made staying
organised your job, forever, and one busy fortnight was enough to kill it.

This is the session where your hands come off the filing.

## What it covers

- A messy source goes in. A clean, properly filed note comes out. Every single
  move is narrated as it happens, so nothing is magic and nothing is off-stage.
- Then further: **one new source updating several pages at once.** That is the
  step that turns a pile of notes into a living wiki that keeps itself current.
- The deal, in full and in plain terms: it reads, it never edits your original,
  it proposes before it acts, it shows you exactly what changed, and you get the
  last word every time.

## What to bring

The mess you already have. A folder of half-finished notes, a notes app you
gave up on, a pile of meeting scribbles. It is more useful to watch this happen
to real material than to a tidy example.

## Before then

Point an AI at this folder and get used to talking to it, so week three is about
the filing rather than the setup. That is [[CONNECT-AN-AI]].
