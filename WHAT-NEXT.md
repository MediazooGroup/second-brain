# What next

You have a folder, two notes and an index. That is further than most people
ever get, and it took an hour.

## What to actually do this week

Not more setup. More writing. In this order.

1. **Write three notes.** Not good ones. Three questions you actually have,
   one file each, whatever you know about them today.
2. **Link two of them.** You will feel the moment it starts working.
3. **Put front matter on all three.** Then find them by clicking a tag.
4. **Come back in a week** and see whether the folder was still there when you
   needed it.

The folder is not the point. The habit of putting a thought somewhere it can be
found again is the point. See [[The north star]].
