# Part 4 — growing it, from you to your whole business

**Wed 19 Aug, 1pm BST. Forty five minutes. The finale.**

> **Filename note.** This note is still called `part-4-keep-it-alive` so the
> links pointing at it from across the brain keep working. The concept it
> describes changed on 12 August; the name did not.

## The promise

The first three weeks were about one person's second brain. Yours. This one is
the same idea at three more sizes, and it is where the series stops being a
personal productivity habit and starts being a way a business remembers things.

## What it teaches

- **The problem is storage, not people.** Ask ten people what the company
  stands for and you get ten answers. The strategy is in a deck from last year.
  The campaign knowledge left with the person who ran it. Nobody can say which
  version of the deliverable is current.
- **Four sizes, one habit.** Personal, project, departmental, organisational.
  The habit does not change. Only where you point it.
- **The organisational brain.** Strategy and OKRs, proposition, sales,
  marketing, product, governance, research, brand. The whole company's shape in
  plain files.
- **The departmental brain.** One function that runs on process rather than on
  whoever happens to work there this year.
- **The project brain.** One client, one deliverable, one deadline. The
  shortest life and the sharpest payoff, and the easiest place to start.
- **They link upward and downward.** A department's plan cites the company's
  OKRs; a project reaches back to the company's brand kit. That is why a
  question asked of one gets an answer that reasons across all three, and why a
  department stops quietly drifting from what the company said it was doing.

## The shape of the hour

Three fast recaps of weeks one to three, so somebody arriving cold loses
nothing. Then three brains, eight minutes each, each opened in Obsidian and
questioned live. Then all four sizes together, and where to start.

## What lands in the viewer's hands

Three complete example brains they can download and take apart — an
organisation, a department, and a project running at a deadline — plus the
ability to argue for one inside their own business.

## Where it points next

Nowhere further in the series. This is the last part. It points at the free
course, at the downloadable kit, and at a conversation about where a second
brain fits in the viewer's own business.
