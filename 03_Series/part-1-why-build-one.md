# Part 1 — why build one

**Wed 29 Jul, 1pm BST. 30 minutes. The intro.**

## The promise

Your mind is for having ideas, not for holding them. In this first session we
see what a second brain is, why now is the moment it finally works, and we watch
a real one in action — this very brain.

## The shape (30 minutes)

Hook → north star → walk a real second brain (the live demo) → what the series
gives you → CTA.

## What it teaches

- **Beat 1 and 2 in full:** information is everywhere; almost none of it
  compounds. Names the pain the viewer feels.
- **A first taste of beats 3 and 4:** a second brain is a compounding system, not
  a folder; and AI turns it from a filing cabinet into a thinking partner.
- **The hundred-year story:** this is not a new fad. From commonplace books to
  Vannevar Bush's memex to today. See [[06_Sources/hundred-year-story]].

## The centrepiece

A live walk of a real second brain — folder structure, one file, the frontmatter
at the top — then questions asked of it live. (You are looking at that brain
right now.)

## The one quick win

One ten-minute thing the viewer can do today: capture three ideas they already
have into three plain notes. That is a second brain, begun.
