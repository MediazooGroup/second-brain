# Part 2 — your first vault

**Wed 5 Aug, 1pm BST. One hour.**

## The promise

If "markdown" and "vault" make you think this is for technical people, this one
is for you. We start from absolute zero and you leave with your first real note
and a home for it, shaped around your own questions.

## What it teaches

- **Beat 5 (the enabler): structure.** Markdown is ordinary writing plus about
  five small marks — a hash for a heading, two stars for bold, a dash for a list.
  That is the whole "technical" part.
- **The ground under beat 3:** why plain files you own beat any app — no lock-in,
  searchable forever, readable in ten years.
- We write a real note live, on a question that actually matters, then explore a
  fully built second brain and pull out the handful of rules that make it work.

## What lands in the viewer's hands

Their first note, and a home for it, in files they own — shaped around their own
questions, not someone else's system.
