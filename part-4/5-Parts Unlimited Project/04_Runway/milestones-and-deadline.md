# Milestones and the deadline

## The deadline

**Wednesday 30 September 2026.** Six weeks and one day from today.

It is immovable because Parts Unlimited holds its **annual operations conference on Thursday 8 October**, and the aftermarket operations director is presenting the programme's early results there.

**What is actually due on 30 September:**

- All three cohorts complete — 48 delegates, both sessions.
- The **interim** outcome report, with the in-session evidence and cohort 1's three-week read.
- An honest statement of what is not yet known.

**What is not due on 30 September:** the full outcome report and the day-30 measure. Those land on **Friday 30 October**, because cohort 3's second session is on 29 September and day 30 is arithmetic, not negotiation.

That distinction is the most misunderstood thing on this programme and the reason [[5-Parts Unlimited Project/06_Log/risks-and-issues|R1]] exists.

## The critical path

Only two things can move the 30 September date, and both are in the next ten days:

1. **The IT security sign-off, due 26 August.** Without a written data rule we cannot teach K3, and without K3 we cannot open a laptop in session one. This is the single biggest exposure on the programme.
2. **The baseline, due 28 August.** It is a hard gate in [[3-Acme Corp/05_Product/delivery-model|the delivery model]]: no baseline, no session dates. Only the delivery director can waive it, and on this programme — the internal test case for the KR3.2 fix — a waiver would defeat the point of running it.

After 1 September there is **no slack whatsoever**. Every cohort date is the last possible one that still leaves a week between sessions before 29 September. A cohort that slips cannot be re-run inside the deadline; it moves to October and the interim report goes out covering two cohorts instead of three.

## What we would do if a cohort slipped

Agreed at kick-off so nobody has to invent it under pressure: **the interim report goes out on 30 September regardless**, covering whichever cohorts have completed, and says plainly which one has not. We do not move the report and we do not pad it. See [[3-Acme Corp/08_Brand/voice-and-tone|voice and tone]], rule 4.
