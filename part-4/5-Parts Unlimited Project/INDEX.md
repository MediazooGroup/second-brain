# Parts Unlimited AI training programme

The project brain. One programme, one client, one deadline: **Wednesday 30 September 2026**.

[[3-Acme Corp/INDEX|Acme Corp]] is delivering an AI adoption programme to **Parts Unlimited** — 48 delegates across 60 branches, three cohorts, six half-days through September. The number it lives or dies on is quote follow-up time: **25.4 minutes now, under 10 by day 30.**

This brain exists for the length of the programme and no longer. It holds what is true about **this delivery**. How Acme works in general lives upstairs in [[3-Acme Corp/INDEX|the organisational brain]] and is linked, never restated — the brand rules, the voice, the delivery model and the KSB standard all come from there.

## Why this brain is shaped differently

It is not the shape of the organisational or the departmental brain, and that is deliberate. A project brain is **time-boxed, deadline-shaped and disposable**: brief, people, deliverables, runway, stakeholders, log. Everything in it is either something we promised, someone we promised it to, or a record of what happened.

On 30 October it stops being a working brain. Anything general — what we learned about this client, what we would change in the design — is promoted up into [[3-Acme Corp/INDEX|the organisational brain]] at close ([[3-Acme Corp/05_Product/quality-bar|the four questions at close]]), the outcome story goes to [[4-Acme Marketing/05_Campaigns/marketing-plays|marketing]], and the rest is archived.
