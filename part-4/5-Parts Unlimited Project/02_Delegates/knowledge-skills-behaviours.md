# Knowledge, skills and behaviours

What has to be different about these 48 people on a Tuesday in October. Written **backwards** — behaviours first, then the skills that sustain them, then the smallest amount of knowledge needed to perform those skills. That order is the Acme standard, not a preference: [[3-Acme Corp/05_Product/learning-design-standard|the learning design standard]].

## Behaviours — the only layer the client is buying

Three. Observable, habitual, and each one measurable at day 30 by someone who is not us.

**B1 — Files the source, not the summary.**
The actual quote, the actual complaint thread, the actual Friday numbers go into the branch brain unchanged. Not a tidied version, not a summary written for us.
*Evidence:* sources added to the branch brain in the last fortnight, counted by the regional manager.

**B2 — Checks the answer against the file it came from, before anything goes to a customer.**
Every draft that leaves the branch has been checked against its cited source. This is the behaviour that makes the whole thing safe.
*Evidence:* spot-check of ten outgoing follow-ups per region against their cited sources.

**B3 — Asks the brain before asking a colleague.**
The first move on "what did we quote them last time?" or "how did we handle this before?" is the brain, not the person at the next desk.
*Evidence:* self-report at day 30, plus the regional manager's read.

Three, not five. More than three and none of them land.

## Skills — what someone can do, unaided, on their own work

**S1 — Writes a skill file for a repeated job, in their own words.**
Built in session one, on one of the three prioritised jobs, using their own ten quotes. Sustains B1 and B3.

**S2 — Runs a skill over a real folder of their own work, and fixes it when the output is wrong.**
The fixing is the skill. First output is always mediocre; the delegate who edits the skill file rather than giving up is the one still using it in October. Sustains B3.

**S3 — Checks an answer against its cited source and corrects it.**
Deliberately taught as a *skill*, not as a warning. Delegates practise catching a wrong answer on purpose, in session one, in the room. Sustains B2.

**S4 — Adds a new source to the branch brain without breaking its structure.**
Where it goes, what gets stripped, what never goes in. Sustains B1.

## Knowledge — deliberately small

**K1 — What a second brain is, and what it is not.** Fifteen minutes, once.
**K2 — What markdown is and why plain text.** Ten minutes. Enough to not be frightened of a `.md` file.
**K3 — Which customer data may and may not go into the tool.** The single most important thing taught all day. Names, account numbers, vehicle registrations and pricing agreements are handled per the rule agreed with their IT security lead. Taught in session one, before anything is opened, and repeated in session two.
**K4 — What a skill file is, and what makes a good one.** Ten minutes, then straight into building one.
**K5 — Where the branch brain lives and who maintains it.** Two minutes. Matters at day 30.

The knowledge list is shorter than the skills list. If it is ever longer, the design is wrong.

## The map — what builds what

| Session | Builds | Toward |
|---|---|---|
| **Session 1, first half** | K1, K2, K3, K4 | Ground rules and safety |
| **Session 1, second half** | S1, S3 | B1, B2 |
| **The week between** | S2, on real work, alone | B3 |
| **Session 2, first half** | What broke, fixed together — S2 again | B3 |
| **Session 2, second half** | S4, K5, and the manager's handover | B1, sustained |

The week between the sessions is part of the design. Delegates run their skill on real work with nobody watching, and session two opens with what went wrong. That hour is where the behaviour is actually formed.

## How this connects to the number

B2 and B3 are what make quote follow-up faster without making it worse. A follow-up drafted in three minutes and sent unchecked is not a win; it is a complaint in a fortnight. The measure in [[5-Parts Unlimited Project/01_Brief/scope-and-success|scope and success]] is paired with the day-30 behaviour check for exactly that reason.
