# Who is in the room

**48 delegates. Three cohorts of 16. Two half-days each, a week apart.** Roles and counts only — the named lists live in Parts Unlimited's own systems, not in this brain ([[3-Acme Corp/06_Governance/ways-of-working|ways of working]]).

## The 48, by role

| Role | Count | What they do all day | The job they came for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Branch managers | 20 | Run a trade counter and a small team; interrupted constantly | The Friday summary, and quote chasing |
| Aftermarket sales leads | 14 | Quote, chase, and close with garages and fleets | Quote follow-up, all day |
| Operations supervisors | 8 | Stock, fulfilment and branch process across a cluster | The weekly summary |
| Customer service team leads | 6 | First line for complaints and order problems | Complaint first response |

Every one of them is front line. **Nobody from head office is in these cohorts**, which is deliberate — head office already adopted, and the plateau is in the branches.

## The three cohorts

| Cohort | Region | Session 1 | Session 2 | Size |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **C1** | North | Tue 1 September | Tue 8 September | 16 |
| **C2** | Midlands | Thu 10 September | Thu 17 September | 16 |
| **C3** | South & Ireland | Tue 22 September | Tue 29 September | 16 |

Half-days, 09:00–12:30, delivered on site in a regional hub rather than remotely. Both distribution centres are out of scope.

## Why the cohorts are split this way

**By region, mixed by role** — not by role, which is what the client originally asked for.

A branch manager and their own aftermarket sales lead are in the same room on the same day. It costs a little in role-specific depth and it buys the thing that actually matters: the two people who have to keep the habit going work it out together, in front of each other, and neither can quietly drop it afterwards.

Logged as [[5-Parts Unlimited Project/06_Log/decisions|D3]].

## What each delegate brings

Stated in the joining instructions and repeated at the top of session one:

- A laptop they normally work on, logged in.
- **Ten of their own real quotes** from the last fortnight — messy, ordinary ones, not their best.
- One complaint thread they handled recently, for the customer service leads.
- Last Friday's summary, for the branch managers and supervisors.

Nobody works on demo data at any point. That is a standard, not a preference ([[3-Acme Corp/05_Product/quality-bar|the quality bar]]).

## Release from the floor

The single hardest logistic on this programme, and the thing the regional managers care about more than the content.

- **Cost per delegate: two half-days, plus roughly an hour between sessions.** That number is stated plainly, up front, and has not moved.
- Branch cover is arranged by the regional managers, not by us.
- C3 runs in the last week of September, which collides with the start of their autumn trading peak. It is the reason C3 is the cohort most at risk of attrition — see [[5-Parts Unlimited Project/06_Log/risks-and-issues|R3]].
