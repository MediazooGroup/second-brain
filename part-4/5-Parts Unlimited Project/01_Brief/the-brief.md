# The brief

What we are doing for Parts Unlimited, why, and what "finished" means. Locked at kick-off on 5 August. Anything that changes it goes through [[5-Parts Unlimited Project/06_Log/decisions|the decisions log]], not through a conversation.

## The client, in three lines

Parts Unlimited distributes automotive parts to independent garages, fleet operators and trade counters. 4,200 people, 60 branches and two distribution centres across the UK and Ireland. The money is made on the aftermarket side, where a garage asks for a price and whoever chases the quote fastest usually wins the job.

## Why we are here

They rolled AI licences out to the whole organisation in January 2026, with a launch email and a lunchtime webinar. By April, monthly active use had settled at **14%**, almost all of it in head office. The 60 branches — where the revenue is — had not touched it.

The aftermarket operations director did not want another awareness campaign. He wanted **quote follow-up time down**, a number his team already reports weekly by branch.

## What we are delivering

An AI adoption programme, **phase one, in aftermarket operations only**. Three cohorts, 48 delegates, closing on **Wednesday 30 September 2026**.

The programme changes three jobs, chosen in the prioritisation half-day on 12 August out of a list of nineteen candidates:

1. **Quote follow-up** — the chase message after a quote goes out. Around 25 minutes each, roughly 40 quotes a week per branch.
2. **The branch weekly summary** — the Friday note to the regional manager. Around 90 minutes, every week, in every branch.
3. **Customer complaint first response** — the first reply to a complaint. Slow, and inconsistent between branches.

The other sixteen candidates are written down in priority order and handed over. Several are better phase-two work than anything on this list.

## The constraint everything is designed around

**Customer data does not leave the Parts Unlimited environment.** This came from their IT security lead in the second sales meeting, before anything was designed, which is why it is a design input rather than a crisis. It shapes where the branch brain lives, what goes into it, and what delegates are taught in session one — see [[5-Parts Unlimited Project/02_Delegates/knowledge-skills-behaviours|K3]].

## Why the date is immovable

Parts Unlimited holds its annual operations conference on **Thursday 8 October**. The aftermarket operations director is presenting the programme's early results there. That is the whole reason 30 September is a hard date and not a target — see [[5-Parts Unlimited Project/04_Runway/milestones-and-deadline|the runway]] for what is actually due on it, which is not the full outcome report.

## Who wanted what

- **The operations director** wants the number down and his people back on the floor. He is the economic owner.
- **The L&D director** wants a programme that survives October, and something she can take to her exec that is not attendance.
- **The IT security lead** wants a clear, written answer about customer data that she does not have to re-litigate every week.
- **The regional managers** want to know exactly how many hours this costs them, per person.
