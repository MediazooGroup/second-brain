# Scope and success

What is in, what is out, and the number this whole programme is judged on.

## In scope

- Aftermarket operations only. 48 delegates across 60 branches, in three cohorts.
- The three prioritised jobs in [[5-Parts Unlimited Project/01_Brief/the-brief|the brief]].
- A branch-level second brain, seeded with the material those three jobs need.
- Six half-day sessions, two per cohort, a week apart.
- The delegate handbook, the manager's guide, and the outcome reporting.
- The baseline capture, as a named deliverable with a date.

## Out of scope — written down so it stops being asked

- **No integration with the quoting or stock systems.** We do not touch their software.
- **No licence procurement.** They buy; we advise.
- **No distribution centres.** Both DCs are phase two, and the operations director has been told this in writing twice.
- **No train-the-trainer.** Option for phase two, priced separately, not included here.
- **No content translation.** All material is English.
- **No changes to their complaint-handling policy.** We change how the first response gets drafted, not what the policy says.

Anything on this list that arrives as a request goes to [[5-Parts Unlimited Project/06_Log/decisions|the decisions log]] and is answered in writing within two days. It is not agreed in a session.

## The measure

Agreed with the aftermarket operations director on **5 August**, before anything was designed.

**Primary — quote follow-up time.**

| | Value |
|---|---|
| What | Minutes from quote issued to follow-up message sent, per quote |
| Baseline | **25.4 minutes**, mean across a sample of 40 quotes per region |
| Baseline captured | 17–28 August, by Parts Unlimited's own operations analyst |
| Target | **Under 10 minutes**, same method, same sample size |
| Re-measured | Interim in the final session week; full at day 30 |

**Secondary — sustained use.** 60% of delegates using at least one of their skills in a normal working week, measured at day 30 by their regional manager, not by us.

**Not the measure:** session feedback scores, attendance, completion, or licence activation counts. None of them are reported to anybody who matters.

## Why the baseline is a deliverable

Because [[3-Acme Corp/01_Strategy/okrs-2026|KR3.2]] sits at 41% across the business, and every single miss traces back to a baseline that nobody owned. On this programme the baseline has a window, a method, a named owner on the client side, and a gate: **no baseline, no session dates** ([[3-Acme Corp/05_Product/delivery-model|the delivery model]]).

This is the first Acme engagement built that way. It is being watched internally for exactly that reason.

## What "finished" means

Three things, all of them, or the programme is not closed:

1. All 48 delegates have completed both sessions and hold at least one working skill of their own.
2. The interim outcome report is with the client on **30 September**.
3. The full outcome report, with the day-30 measure, is with the client on **30 October**.

Point 3 falls after the date everyone is talking about. That gap is the biggest risk on this programme — see [[5-Parts Unlimited Project/06_Log/risks-and-issues|R1]].
