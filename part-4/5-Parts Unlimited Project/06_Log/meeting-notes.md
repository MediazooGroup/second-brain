# Meeting notes

Append only, newest last. Roles not names. Anything that changes what somebody does gets lifted into [[5-Parts Unlimited Project/06_Log/decisions|the decisions log]] the same day — these notes are the record of the conversation, not the record of the decision.

## 5 August — Kick-off

**Present:** operations director, L&D director, IT security lead, Acme account director and programme lead.

The operations director opened by saying he had "no interest in a training programme" and wanted the quote follow-up number down. Useful, and worth repeating to anybody joining the programme late: this is not a learning engagement in his mind, and material written in learning language will lose him.

The measure was agreed in the first twenty minutes because he already reports it weekly. Baseline of 25.4 minutes came from his own reporting, not from us.

The IT security lead was clear and constructive. Her position: customer data does not leave the environment, and she is not against the programme, she is against re-litigating the same question every month. She asked for a single written rule she could point at.

The L&D director's concern was different: she has been through two initiatives that "went quiet in November" and wants to know what happens after the sessions. She was reassured by the manager's guide and the day-30 measure, and specifically asked that the managers get their material *before* their people finish.

**Lifted to decisions:** D2, D4.

## 12 August — Prioritisation half-day

**Present:** operations director, three regional managers, six branch representatives, Acme programme lead and learning designer.

Nineteen candidate jobs on the wall. Three chosen: quote follow-up, the branch weekly summary, complaint first response.

The Friday summary was the surprise. We expected quoting to dominate; the branch managers were far more animated about the ninety minutes they lose every Friday writing up numbers that already exist somewhere. One said, roughly, "I'm typing out things the system already knows." That sentence is the best line we have for the outcome story if this lands.

The operations director tried, twice, to add a fourth job. Both are on the handed-over list in position. He accepted it without much resistance once he saw the other sixteen written down in order — the list was worth more to him than any single addition.

**Lifted to decisions:** D6.

## 14 August — Design walkthrough

**Present:** L&D director, Acme learning designer and programme lead.

Signed off in the meeting. Two changes she asked for, both good:

- Move the data rule earlier, so it is taught before anything opens. Done — it is now at 09:40 in session one and delivered jointly with a Parts Unlimited representative wherever possible.
- Add something explicit for the customer service leads, who felt like an afterthought in the draft. Done, in the session-two second-job slot.

She raised the branding question about the handbook herself, which is where D5 came from.

**Lifted to decisions:** D5, D7.

**Lifted to decisions:** D8. **Lifted to risks:** R2 revised, R3 confirmed.
