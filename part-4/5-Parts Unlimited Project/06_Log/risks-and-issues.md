## Risks — ranked by what they would cost

### R1 — The interim report is treated as the outcome, and the day-30 measure never happens

**High likelihood · High impact · Owner: programme lead**

The conference is on 8 October. The full measure lands on 30 October. Once the operations director has presented, the pull to consider the programme finished is enormous — and this is exactly how Acme's [[3-Acme Corp/01_Strategy/okrs-2026|KR3.2]] got to 41%.

**Mitigation:** the 30 October date is in the contract as a deliverable, the day-30 re-measure is booked in their analyst's calendar for 28 October, and the interim report opens by saying what it is not. The content lead is in the outcome-report conversation, not brought in afterwards.

**This is the biggest risk on the programme and it is not a delivery risk. It is a follow-through risk.**

### R2 — Baseline not signed off by 28 August

**Medium · High · Owner: their operations analyst, chased by the programme lead**

No baseline, no session dates — a hard gate in [[3-Acme Corp/05_Product/delivery-model|the delivery model]]. Waiving it needs the delivery director, and on the programme that is the internal test case for the KR3.2 fix, a waiver would defeat the point.

**Mitigation:** sample size cut from 60 to 40 quotes per region on 17 August to make the window achievable. Method already matches their weekly reporting, so this is collation, not new measurement.

### R3 — Cohort 3 attrition in the autumn trading peak

**Medium · Medium · Owner: the South & Ireland regional manager, by 25 August**

Cohort 3 runs 22 and 29 September, into the start of their peak. Branch cover is hardest exactly then, and the second session is the one that gets dropped.

**Mitigation:** moving cohort 3 earlier is open in [[5-Parts Unlimited Project/06_Log/decisions|the decisions log]]. If it cannot move, over-recruit by two and confirm cover four weeks out rather than two.

### R4 — Delegates arrive without their own material

**Medium · Medium · Owner: lead facilitator**

The design depends on real quotes from hour one. A room of people with nothing to work on becomes a demo, which is the thing we do not do.

**Mitigation:** joining instructions say it twice; regional managers reminded the week before; facilitator carries a fallback set of *anonymised Parts Unlimited* material, never generic examples — and using it is recorded as a design failure, not a save.

### R5 — Relationship concentrated in two people

**Low · High · Owner: account director**

The operations director and the L&D director carry the programme and phase two between them. Also on [[3-Acme Corp/03_Sales/accounts/parts-unlimited|the account risk register]].

**Mitigation:** deliberately widen to the regional managers and the internal comms manager during September, which the comms conversation at cohort 3 already does.

### R6 — Scope creep from the sessions

**Medium · Low · Owner: lead facilitator**

Delegates will ask for the fourth, fifth and sixth job. It is a good sign and it is still scope creep.

**Mitigation:** never agreed in the room. Noted, thanked, into the log, answered in writing within two days ([[5-Parts Unlimited Project/01_Brief/scope-and-success|scope and success]]).
