# Decisions log

Append only. Every decision that changes what somebody else does: what was decided, by which role, when, and — the part that earns its keep — **what it closes off**.

The point is not the audit trail. It is that in week five nobody reopens the thing settled in week one.

---

## D1 — Phase one is aftermarket operations only

**28 July · Account director with the operations director**

Both distribution centres are out of phase one, along with head office.

**Closes off:** "could we just add the DCs?" The answer is phase two, priced separately. Asked twice since and answered from this entry both times.

---

## D2 — The measure is quote follow-up time, and nothing else

**5 August · Operations director, at kick-off**

25.4-minute baseline, target under 10, same method and sample as their weekly reporting. Sustained use at day 30 is a secondary read.

**Closes off:** session feedback scores, attendance and licence activation as measures of success. Changing this now needs delivery director approval ([[3-Acme Corp/06_Governance/decision-rights|decision rights]]).

---

## D3 — Cohorts split by region, mixed by role

**7 August · Programme lead with the three regional managers**

The client originally asked for role-based cohorts. We proposed regional, mixed by role, so a branch manager and their own sales lead are in the same room.

**Why:** less role-specific depth, far better odds the habit survives, because the two people who have to sustain it work it out in front of each other.

**Closes off:** re-cutting the cohort lists. Lists were confirmed on 11 August.

---

## D4 — Customer data does not leave the Parts Unlimited environment

**5 August · IT security lead, confirmed at kick-off**

The branch brain lives inside their environment. The rule about names, account numbers, vehicle registrations and pricing agreements is taught as K3, before any laptop opens.

**Closes off:** any tooling suggestion that moves data out. **Note:** the written sign-off is still outstanding and is on the critical path — [[5-Parts Unlimited Project/04_Runway/milestones-and-deadline|26 August]].

## D6 — Three prioritised jobs, sixteen written down and handed over

**12 August · Prioritisation half-day, operations director present**

Quote follow-up, the branch weekly summary, complaint first response. The other sixteen candidates are ordered, written up and handed over.

## D7 — No live tooling demo of anything they do not already have licences for

**14 August · Programme lead**

Sessions use only what a delegate can open on Monday morning.

**Closes off:** showing something better that they cannot use. It creates want, not behaviour, and it undermines D4.

## D8 — The interim report goes out on 30 September regardless

**17 August · Programme lead with the account director**

If a cohort slips, the 30 September report covers whichever cohorts completed and says plainly which one did not. We do not move the date and we do not pad it.

**Closes off:** a late-September argument about whether to delay, held under pressure with a conference four days away.
