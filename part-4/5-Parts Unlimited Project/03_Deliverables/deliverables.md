# Deliverables

**Six.** Each one has an owner, a date and a definition of done. Nothing on this list is "in progress" without a date beside it.

| # | Deliverable | Owner | Due | State |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | Baseline measurement | Parts Unlimited operations analyst | 28 Aug | In progress |
| D2 | Aftermarket branch brain, seeded | Acme learning designer | 26 Aug | In progress |
| D3 | Six half-day sessions | Acme lead facilitator | 1–29 Sept | Not started |
| D4 | Delegate handbook | Acme learning designer | 26 Aug | Draft with client |
| D5 | Manager's 30-day guide | Acme programme lead | 7 Sept | Not started |
| D6 | Outcome report — interim, then full | Acme programme lead | 30 Sept / 30 Oct | Not started |

## D1 — Baseline measurement

Quote follow-up time across a sample of 40 quotes per region, measured by **their** analyst, not ours, using the method already used for weekly reporting.

**Done when:** a number, a date, a written method, and the operations director's sign-off. **Gate:** no baseline, no session dates. Details in [[5-Parts Unlimited Project/01_Brief/scope-and-success|scope and success]].

## D2 — The aftermarket branch brain

The folder structure and seeded content the three prioritised jobs need: past quotes and outcomes, the parts and supersession notes branches keep re-explaining, the complaint templates, the tone rules for talking to a garage.

Hosted inside the Parts Unlimited environment. **No customer data leaves it** — the constraint the whole programme is designed around.

**Done when:** structure agreed with the L&D director, seeded with real material, and a delegate who has never seen it can find last quarter's quote for a named account inside a minute.

## D3 — Six half-day sessions

Two per cohort, a week apart. Design in [[5-Parts Unlimited Project/03_Deliverables/programme-design|programme design]].

**Done when:** every delegate has completed both sessions and holds at least one working skill of their own that has run on a real job.

## D4 — Delegate handbook

Their reference after the room. Not slides. Written in the delegate's language: what to do on Monday, the data rule, how to fix a skill that has stopped being useful, and who to ask.

**Branded to Parts Unlimited**, not to Acme. Acme appears once, small, in the footer. That is the co-branding rule in [[4-Acme Marketing/08_Brand/brand-guidelines|the brand guidelines]], and it was a live decision on this programme rather than an assumption — logged as [[5-Parts Unlimited Project/06_Log/decisions|D5]].

**Done when:** approved by the L&D director and printed for cohort 1.

## D5 — Manager's 30-day guide

For the three regional managers and the branch managers of anyone in a cohort. Four short conversations to have across 30 days, what to look for, and how to spot a delegate quietly abandoning it.

**Acme-branded**, because it is method rather than client material.

**Done when:** issued to all managers before cohort 1's session two, and walked through live with the regional managers.

## D6 — Outcome report

Two documents, and the difference between them is the most misunderstood thing on this programme.

- **Interim, 30 September.** All cohorts complete, the in-session evidence, cohort 1's three-week read, and the honest position on what is not yet known. Written for the operations conference on 8 October.
- **Full, 30 October.** The day-30 measure against the baseline, the behaviour evidence, what did not work, and the handover.

**Done when:** written *with* the client, seen by the operations director, and — separately — the case-study ask has been made in the same conversation. See [[4-Acme Marketing/05_Campaigns/marketing-plays|play 1]].

## What is not a deliverable

Slides. Attendance certificates. A licence usage dashboard. A completion report. If any of these are asked for, the answer is in [[5-Parts Unlimited Project/01_Brief/scope-and-success|scope]].
