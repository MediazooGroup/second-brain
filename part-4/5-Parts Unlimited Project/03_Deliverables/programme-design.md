# Programme design — the six sessions

Two half-days per cohort, a week apart, built backwards from the behaviours in [[5-Parts Unlimited Project/02_Delegates/knowledge-skills-behaviours|knowledge, skills and behaviours]]. Same design for all three cohorts; the examples change with the region.

## Session one — 09:00 to 12:30

**09:00 — The number.** Opens with their own baseline: 25.4 minutes per quote follow-up, and what that adds up to across a region in a week. No introductions to Acme, no agenda slide.

**09:15 — What this is and is not (K1, K2).** Twenty-five minutes, total. A second brain is a folder of your own material that an assistant can read. Markdown is a text file with a few marks in it. That is the entire theory content of the programme.

**09:40 — The data rule (K3).** The most important half hour of the day, and it happens *before* anything is opened. What may go in, what never does, and what to do when you are not sure. Delivered jointly with a Parts Unlimited representative wherever possible, so it is their rule and not ours.

**10:20 — Break.**

**10:35 — Build a skill (K4, S1).** Delegates open their own ten quotes and write a skill file for their own follow-up job, in their own words. Not from a template. The facilitator's job here is to stop people writing what they think we want.

**11:40 — Break it on purpose (S3).** Every delegate makes the assistant produce a wrong answer, catches it against the source, and corrects it. Taught as a skill to practise, never as a warning to fear. This is the hour that makes B2 survive contact with a busy Tuesday.

**12:10 — The week's task.** Run it on real work. Bring back what broke. Nothing else.

## The week between

The part of the design that people mistake for a gap. Each delegate uses their skill on live work, alone, with nobody watching — which is the only realistic simulation of October.

They are asked for one thing: **note what broke.** A shared page in the branch brain collects it, and the facilitator reads it the day before session two, so session two is built on what actually happened rather than what we planned.

## Session two — 09:00 to 12:30

**09:00 — What broke.** Straight into the list from the week. No recap. The most common three get fixed together, on screen, in the room.

**09:45 — Fix your own (S2).** Delegates edit their own skill files against what went wrong. The message that matters: the first version is always mediocre, and editing it is the job, not a sign it failed.

**10:45 — Break.**

**11:00 — Feeding the brain (S4, K5).** Adding a new source without breaking the structure. Where it lives, who maintains it, what gets stripped out first.

**11:45 — The second job.** A quick pass at one of the other two prioritised jobs, to prove the method transfers rather than to master it.

**12:10 — What happens next.** The 30-day check, who their manager is, and what will be measured. The manager's guide is already in their manager's hands by this point, on purpose.

## Facilitation notes

- **Their files, from the first hour.** No demo data at any point, ever.
- **Interruption is normal.** These are branch people; phones will go. Design for it — nothing runs longer than 35 minutes without a task.
- **Do not defend the technology.** When someone says it got something wrong, agree, and go to S3.
- **Never agree scope in the room.** Note it, thank them, put it in [[5-Parts Unlimited Project/06_Log/decisions|the decisions log]].
- **Facilitator notes go into this brain the same day**, not at the end of the cohort ([[3-Acme Corp/05_Product/quality-bar|the quality bar]]).

## What we cut from the first draft

- A tools comparison. Interesting, changes nothing, twenty minutes.
- A prompting techniques section. It was knowledge dressed as a skill.
- A group exercise on "AI opportunities in your branch". It produced a wish list, and the prioritisation is already done.
