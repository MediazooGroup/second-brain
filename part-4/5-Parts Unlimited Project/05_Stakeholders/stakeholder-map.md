# Stakeholder map

Who matters on this programme, what they need, and what happens if they are ignored. **Roles only** — names live in the client's systems, not in this brain ([[3-Acme Corp/06_Governance/ways-of-working|ways of working]]).

## Parts Unlimited

| Role | Interest | What they need from us | If ignored |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Aftermarket operations director** | **Economic owner.** Holds the budget and the number | The number down, his people back on the floor, no surprises before 8 October | The programme loses its sponsor and phase two never happens |
| **L&D director** | Programme sponsor, day to day | Something that survives October and is not attendance | Delivery detail stops flowing and cohort logistics fall apart |
| **IT security lead** | Gate on the data rule | A clear written answer she does not have to re-litigate | Session one cannot open a laptop. This is the critical path |
| **Internal comms manager** | Amplifier | The delegate story, in their people's words, unbranded | The outcome never travels internally and there is no case study |
| **Three regional managers** | Release the delegates | Exact hours, cover arranged early, and the 30-day guide | Attrition, especially cohort 3 in the trading peak |
| **Operations analyst** | Owns the baseline and the day-30 measure | A method they already use, not a new one | No baseline, no gate, no proof |

## Acme Corp

| Role | Interest | Accountable for |
|---|---|---|
| **Account director** | The account and phase two | Contracted scope and dates. Nothing changes without them |
| **Programme lead** | Delivery end to end | The runway, the decisions log, D5 and D6 |
| **Lead facilitator** | The room | D3, and facilitator notes filed the same day |
| **Learning designer** | The design | D2 and D4, and the KSB map |
| **Content lead** | The outcome story | Present at the outcome-report conversation, not after it |

## The three that decide whether this works

**The operations director**, because he owns the number and the conference date. Weekly, one page, no surprises.

**The IT security lead**, because she is the critical path. She was in the second sales meeting, before anything was designed, which is why her constraint shaped the programme instead of derailing it. Keep her in the loop even in the weeks when there is nothing to ask her.

**The regional managers**, because they are the only reason 48 people walk into a room. They care about hours and cover, not about learning design. Talk to them about hours and cover.

## Communication rhythm

- **Weekly, Monday** — one-page status to the operations director and the L&D director. Published as [[5-Parts Unlimited Project/06_Log/status|status]].
- **Fortnightly** — programme call with the L&D director and the regional managers.
- **On change only** — the IT security lead, plus a courtesy note when nothing has changed.
- **Twice** — the internal comms manager: once at cohort 1 to explain what is coming, once after cohort 3 about the story.
- **Never in a session** — no scope agreed in a room. It goes to the log.

## The gap worth naming

The relationship currently rests on two people: the operations director and the L&D director. If either moves before October, the programme is exposed and so is phase two. This is on the account risk register as well as ours — [[3-Acme Corp/03_Sales/accounts/parts-unlimited|the account page]].
