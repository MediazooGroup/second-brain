# Quality bar

What "good" means, phase by phase, in a form somebody can actually check. Used at design review and at close.

## Discover

- The three prioritised jobs are named in the client's language, not ours.
- The jobs we did not pick are written down, in order, with a one-line reason.
- The baseline has a number, a date, a method and a named owner who reports it.
- The security constraint is written as a design constraint, not as a risk.

## Design

- The behaviours are observable acts. Read them aloud; if you cannot picture someone doing it on a Tuesday, rewrite it.
- Every session maps to a skill, and every skill to a behaviour ([[3-Acme Corp/05_Product/learning-design-standard|the standard]]).
- Nothing in the design runs on invented data. Delegate files from hour one.
- The client's business owner has read it and can restate it unprompted.

## Deliver

- Every delegate leaves session one with something of their own that works on one real job.
- The week between sessions has an actual task, and session two opens with what broke.
- Facilitator notes are in the project brain the same day.
- Nothing is promised in the room that is not in scope. Note it, do not agree it.

## Prove

- The measure is taken by the same person, the same way, as the baseline.
- The outcome report says what did not work as well as what did.
- No number is published that we did not measure ourselves.
- Handover material is written for someone who was not there.

## The four questions at close

Asked of every programme, answered in writing, filed in the project brain:

1. Did the number move, and how do we know?
2. What did the delegates keep doing at day 30, and what did they drop?
3. What in the design would we change before running it again?
4. What did we learn about this client that the account team needs?

## The one rule above all of these

**If we cannot prove it, we do not claim it.** Not in a report, not in a proposal, not on a stage. One published outcome story means we say "one" — see [[3-Acme Corp/02_Proposition/differentiators|differentiators, what we do not claim]].
