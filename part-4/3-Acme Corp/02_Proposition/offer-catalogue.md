# Offer catalogue

Three offers. Everything we sell is one of them, or a combination of them. If a request does not fit, it is either a no or a conversation with the leadership team — see [[3-Acme Corp/06_Governance/decision-rights|decision rights]].

| Offer | What the client buys | Typical shape | Objective it serves |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[3-Acme Corp/02_Proposition/offers/ai-adoption-programme\|AI adoption programme]] | People actually using AI on the jobs that eat their week | Two half-days per cohort, then 30 days of embedding | **O1**, and O3 through its measure |
| [[3-Acme Corp/02_Proposition/offers/leadership-development\|Leadership development]] | Managers who can lead a change rather than announce one | Six touchpoints over a quarter, cohort-based | O2 — it is the offer that most often follows a first programme |
| [[3-Acme Corp/02_Proposition/offers/change-communications\|Change communications]] | The story of the change, told so people believe it | Campaign-shaped, four to eight weeks | O2, and it protects the other two offers |

Objectives are in [[3-Acme Corp/01_Strategy/okrs-2026|OKRs 2026]].

## Which one carries the year

**AI adoption is the flagship.** It is the offer O1 is written about, it is the one clients call us about unprompted, and it is the one with the clearest measure. It is also the newest, which is why its evidence base is thinner than its demand — the reason KR1.3 is the one at risk.

Leadership development is the steady one. It is the second sale in most accounts and it is what keeps a client after an AI programme has landed, because the managers are the ones who have to hold the new habit in place.

Change communications is the smallest of the three by revenue and the one we most often give away inside another programme. It stays in the catalogue because a programme with no story around it dies quietly, and because it is a cheap way into a new department.

## How they combine

The pattern that works, and the one we should be proposing more often:

1. **AI adoption** in one department, with a measure, to prove it.
2. **Change communications** to tell the rest of the organisation what happened, in the client's own words.
3. **Leadership development** for the managers of the next two departments, before their cohorts run.

That sequence is the whole of O2 in three lines. It is what we are aiming at with [[3-Acme Corp/03_Sales/accounts/parts-unlimited|Parts Unlimited]].
