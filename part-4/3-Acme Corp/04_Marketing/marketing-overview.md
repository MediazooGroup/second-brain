# Marketing — the organisational view

This is the **organisation's** view of marketing: what the department is for, what it is accountable for this year, and where the detail lives. The detail itself is not here on purpose.

**The department's own brain is [[4-Acme Marketing/INDEX|4-Acme Marketing]].** Personas, plays, campaigns, content standards and brand guidelines all live there and are maintained there. If you find yourself about to write any of that into this brain, you are in the wrong folder.

## What marketing is for

To make the right buyer arrive already believing the thing we would otherwise have to spend a first meeting proving: that we make change stick, and that we have done it in production for over a year.

## What it is accountable for in 2026

Marketing owns one key result outright and materially affects two more.

| Key result | Marketing's part |
|---|---|
| **KR1.3** — three published client outcome stories | Owned outright. At one of three, and the reason O1's evidence base is thin |
| KR1.1 — AI adoption as 40% of delivered revenue | Demand generation into the AI adoption offer |
| KR2.2 — every top-10 account with a twelve-month plan | The account-based play, run with sales |

Objectives in full: [[3-Acme Corp/01_Strategy/okrs-2026|OKRs 2026]].

## The dependency worth naming

Marketing cannot manufacture KR1.3 on its own. An outcome story needs a measured outcome to exist first, which is delivery's job and currently the constraint ([[3-Acme Corp/01_Strategy/okrs-2026|KR3.2]] at 41%). The agreed working rule since the half-year review: **the outcome report and the case-study ask are the same conversation with the client, at close, not two conversations three months apart.**

## Boundary with the department

- The organisation sets the objectives. The department chooses the plays that serve them.
- Brand guidelines are **owned by marketing** and used by everyone. The org brain holds only the short brand kit at [[3-Acme Corp/08_Brand/brand-kit|08_Brand]]; the full guidelines are at [[4-Acme Marketing/08_Brand/brand-guidelines|4-Acme Marketing/08_Brand]].
- Nothing client-confidential is written into marketing's brain. Account detail stays in [[3-Acme Corp/03_Sales/accounts/parts-unlimited|03_Sales/accounts]].
