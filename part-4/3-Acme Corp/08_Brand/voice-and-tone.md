# Voice and tone

How Acme Corp sounds, in a proposal, in a room, in a delegate handbook and on a post. **Voice does not change. Tone does.**

## The voice, in five rules

1. **Plain.** Short words where they will do. If a sentence needs reading twice, rewrite it.
2. **Specific.** Numbers, names of jobs, actual examples. "The Friday branch summary took ninety minutes" beats "significant efficiency gains" every time — provided the ninety minutes is a number we actually measured.
3. **Direct.** Say the thing. Active voice, second person where possible. We write *you*, not *the delegate*.
4. **Honest about limits.** We say what did not work. One outcome story means we say one. This is the rule that makes the other four believable.
5. **Never hyped.** No "revolutionary", no "game-changing", no "unleash". The work is interesting enough without adjectives.

## Tone by situation

| Where | Tone | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| Proposal | Confident, quiet, specific | Drifting into consultancy noun-stacking |
| Delegate handbook | Warm, encouraging, plain | Talking down; delegates are experts at their own job |
| Session materials | Practical, imperative, short | Explaining the theory before the task |
| Outcome report | Measured, even-handed | Selling. The report is not a proposal |
| Marketing and social | Human, opinionated, generous | Hype, and claiming what we have not measured |
| Bad news to a client | Fast, plain, with the fix beside it | Cushioning. Say it first, explain second |

## Words we use, and words we do not

**We say:** job, habit, week, cohort, delegate, measure, baseline, stick, prove.

**We do not say:** solution, journey, leverage, upskill, transformation programme (when we mean a course), best-in-class, cutting-edge, seamless, empower.

**Careful with:** "AI". Say what it does in the sentence — drafts the follow-up, summarises the call — rather than leaning on the two letters.

## The test

Read it aloud. If you would not say it to a branch manager standing at a trade counter with three minutes to spare, it is not in our voice.
