# Brand kit — the short version

The organisation-wide summary. **The full guidelines, including usage rules, layout, imagery and the things we never do, are owned by marketing: [[4-Acme Marketing/08_Brand/brand-guidelines|Acme Marketing brand guidelines]].** If the two ever disagree, marketing's version wins and this page gets corrected.

## Colour

| Name | Hex | Where it is used |
|---|---|---|
| Acme Deep | `#12233A` | Primary. Text, headers, the logo mark |
| Acme Spark | `#F2A104` | Accent, used sparingly. One highlight per page or slide |
| Acme Sage | `#4C8577` | Secondary. Diagrams, supporting panels |
| Acme Paper | `#F7F6F2` | Background. Not white |
| Acme Slate | `#5A6472` | Body text on paper, captions, secondary labels |

Two colours carry any single piece: Deep and Paper. Spark and Sage are for one thing each, never both at full strength in the same frame.

## Type

- **Headings:** Inter, semibold, tight tracking.
- **Body:** Inter, regular. Long-form documents use Source Serif at body size.
- **Never:** more than two weights in a single document; all-caps for anything longer than a two-word label.

## The mark

The wordmark is "Acme" in Acme Deep with a single Spark underscore. It has clear space of one cap-height on every side, and it is never placed on a photograph, recoloured, outlined, rotated or set inside a shape.

## The motif

A small repeated square that grows across a sequence — one, then four, then nine. It carries the idea in [[3-Acme Corp/01_Strategy/mission|the mission]]: something small, repeated, that becomes the way things are done. It is a motif, not a logo, and it never appears in the same frame as the wordmark.

## When you need more than this page

Anything client-facing — a proposal, a delegate handbook, a deck, a campaign asset — is built from the full guidelines, not from this summary. Delegate-facing material for a client programme also has to sit alongside *their* brand, which is a question the guidelines answer and this page does not.
