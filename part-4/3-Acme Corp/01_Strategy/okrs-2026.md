# OKRs 2026

Three objectives for the year. Nine key results. Reviewed at the end of every quarter; the numbers below carry the **half-year position at 31 July 2026**.

Everything ladders back to [[3-Acme Corp/01_Strategy/mission|the mission]]. If a piece of work does not move one of these nine key results, it needs a very good reason to exist.

## O1 — Make AI adoption our flagship offer

Two years ago AI work was a curiosity in the corner of other programmes. This year it becomes the thing we are known for.

| Key result | Target | At 31 July | Position |
|---|---|---|---|
| KR1.1 — AI adoption programmes as a share of delivered revenue | 40% by Q4 | 28% | On track |
| KR1.2 — Client engagements running the repeatable AI adoption design | 8 | 6 | On track |
| KR1.3 — Published client outcome stories with a measured time saving | 3 | 1 | At risk |

**Where it stands:** the delivery side is healthy and the design is holding up across very different clients. The gap is publication — we are doing the work and not telling anyone about it. KR1.3 is the marketing department's single biggest job for the rest of the year; see [[4-Acme Marketing/01_Strategy/marketing-strategy|the marketing strategy]].

## O2 — Grow the clients we already have

Winning new logos is the expensive way to grow. Second and third programmes inside an account we already understand are the cheap way.

| Key result | Target | At 31 July | Position |
|---|---|---|---|
| KR2.1 — Revenue from clients in their second year or beyond | 60% | 57% | On track |
| KR2.2 — Top-10 accounts with a written twelve-month plan | 10 of 10 | 8 of 10 | On track |
| KR2.3 — Average live programmes per top-10 account | 2.5 | 2.1 | Behind |

**Where it stands:** account plans are getting written, but the second programme is landing late in each account. The pattern we keep seeing is that the second sale needs a proven outcome from the first one, which is the same bottleneck as KR1.3.

## O3 — Prove the outcome, not the delivery

Our clients can already buy delivery. What they cannot easily buy is proof that anything changed.

| Key result | Target | At 31 July | Position |
|---|---|---|---|
| KR3.1 — Programmes with the measure agreed with the client on day one | 100% | 92% | On track |
| KR3.2 — Programmes reporting a measured outcome within 30 days of close | 80% | 41% | **Furthest behind** |
| KR3.3 — Delegates reporting they applied it at work, at day 30 | 70% | 66% | On track |

**Where it stands:** KR3.2 is the furthest behind of all nine key results, and it is the one that blocks the other two. We agree the measure on day one and then lose it, because nobody owns the baseline. The fix agreed at the half-year review: the baseline is captured **before the first session runs**, and it is a named deliverable on the programme, not an afterthought. The Parts Unlimited programme is the first one built that way — see [[5-Parts Unlimited Project/01_Brief/scope-and-success|scope and success]].

## The one-line read for the year

O1 is working, O2 is waiting on O1's proof, and O3 is the thing holding both of them up. **If we only fix one thing this year, it is KR3.2.**
