# Mission

The sentence every proposal, every programme and every piece of marketing ladders back to. If a thing we are about to do does not serve this, we do not do it.

> **We help large organisations turn new ways of working into everyday habits, so change survives the week it launches.**

## The short version

> **We make change stick.**

Use the short version in a room, on a call, on a slide. Use the full version in writing, where the second half of the sentence is doing the work.

## Why this wording, line by line

- **"Large organisations"** — we are built for scale and distribution. Our design, our facilitation model and our measurement all assume hundreds of people across many sites. See [[3-Acme Corp/03_Sales/ideal-client-profile|the ideal client profile]].
- **"New ways of working"** — deliberately broader than "training". We are hired when the way people work has to change, and training is only one of the things that has to happen.
- **"Everyday habits"** — the unit we care about is behaviour on a Tuesday, not a completion certificate. This is why every programme design ends in behaviours, not modules. See [[3-Acme Corp/05_Product/learning-design-standard|the learning design standard]].
- **"Survives the week it launches"** — the failure we exist to prevent. Most change programmes peak on launch day. Ours are measured 30 days later, on purpose.

## What this rules out

The mission is a filter, not a poster. It is why we say no to catalogue e-learning, to bodyshopping, and to any engagement with no measure attached. The full list of things we deliberately do not do is in [[3-Acme Corp/01_Strategy/strategy-on-a-page|strategy on a page]].
