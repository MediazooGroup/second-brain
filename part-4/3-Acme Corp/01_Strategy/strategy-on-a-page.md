# Strategy on a page

Where we play, how we win, and — the part most companies skip — what we have decided not to do.

## Where we play

- **Sector:** organisations of 1,000 people or more, where the workforce is distributed across sites, branches or countries. Regulated sectors are welcome; heavily bespoke software environments are not our ground.
- **Buyer:** an L&D, transformation or operations leader who owns a change and already reports a business number that the change is supposed to move.
- **Geography:** UK and Ireland as home, Western Europe where a client is already ours. We do not open a new country for a first engagement.
- **Problem:** the organisation has decided to work differently and cannot get the new way to stick past launch.

Full profile, with the qualifying questions: [[3-Acme Corp/03_Sales/ideal-client-profile|ideal client profile]].

## How we win

1. **We have done it in production for over a year.** Not a pilot, not a lab. Patterns that survived contact with real branches, real shift patterns and real IT security teams.
2. **We prioritise before we design.** The first half-day is spent working out which handful of jobs are worth changing. Clients arrive with twenty ideas and leave with three.
3. **The measure is agreed on day one.** Before we design a session, the client tells us the number that has to move, and we capture its baseline.
4. **We build with the delegates, not for them.** Every programme leaves working artefacts the delegates made themselves, in their own words, on their own files.
5. **We hand over the method.** The client can run the next cohort without us. That sounds like it costs us work; in practice it is why they come back for the next department.

## What we deliberately do not do

This list is as much a part of the strategy as the rest of it, and it is the answer to most "could we just…" questions.

- **No off-the-shelf e-learning catalogue.** We do not sell library content, and we do not compete on module count.
- **No staff augmentation.** We do not place people into a client's team by the day to do the client's work.
- **No software builds.** We do not write applications, integrate systems, or take on anything that needs a maintenance contract.
- **No engagement without a measure.** If a client will not name the number the work is supposed to move, we do not take the work.
- **No single stand-alone workshop.** A one-day "AI awareness session" with nothing after it is the thing our mission exists to prevent. If that is genuinely all a client wants, we say no and stay friendly.
- **No procurement of licences or tooling on a client's behalf.** We advise; they buy.

## The tension we are managing this year

Demand for AI work is arriving faster than our proof is. The commercial pull is to say yes to everything and worry about evidence later. [[3-Acme Corp/01_Strategy/okrs-2026|The OKRs]] deliberately pull the other way: O3 is what stops O1 turning into a pile of unproven delivery.
