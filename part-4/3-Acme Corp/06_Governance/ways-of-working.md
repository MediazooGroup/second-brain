# Ways of working — how this brain is kept

The rules that keep the organisational brain worth reading. Short, because a maintenance policy nobody reads is a maintenance policy nobody follows.

## What belongs in here

The **organisation's** view: what we believe, what we sell, how we win, how we deliver, how we sound. Things that are true across departments and outlast any one engagement.

## What does not

- **Department detail.** Personas, plays, campaign mechanics and content standards live in [[4-Acme Marketing/INDEX|the marketing brain]] and its equivalents.
- **Live project detail.** Meeting notes, delegate lists, risks and status live in the project's own brain, like [[5-Parts Unlimited Project/INDEX|Parts Unlimited]].
- **Numbers that belong in a system.** Prices, rates, invoices, individual performance. The brain describes how we shape a deal; the finance system holds what it cost. See [[3-Acme Corp/03_Sales/commercial-model|the commercial model]].
- **Anything personal.** No individual names against opinions, no HR matters, no personal data. Roles, not people.
- **Client-confidential material.** Account pages carry what we need to serve the account, in our words. Never the client's confidential documents.

## The conventions

- **Front matter on every note:** title, type, tags, captured, source, status. `reviewed` when it has been checked since.
- **One idea per note.** If a note needs two headings that could each be a page, it is two pages.
- **Wiki-links, not copies.** If a fact is in another note, link to it. A fact written down twice is a fact that will disagree with itself within a quarter.
- **One `INDEX.md` at the root.** Every note appears there. If it is not on the index, it does not exist.
- **Prose, not bullets, for anything that needs a reason.** Bullets are for lists of things, not for arguments.
- **Status is honest.** `locked` means do not change without a decision. `active` means current. `draft` means do not quote it to a client.

## The maintenance loop

- **Weekly:** whoever ran a client conversation adds what they learned to the relevant note, or drops it in the marketing inbox if it is not clear where it goes yet.
- **Quarterly:** OKRs reviewed and the numbers updated in place, with the review date in front matter.
- **At every programme close:** the four questions from [[3-Acme Corp/05_Product/quality-bar|the quality bar]] are answered and anything general is promoted up into this brain.
- **Twice a year:** a read-through for notes nobody has opened. Delete or merge them. A brain grows by pruning as much as by writing.
