# Decision rights

Who decides what, so that nobody has to guess and nothing waits for a meeting that was not needed. **Roles, not people** — the role holds the right, and it moves with the role.

## The default

**The person closest to the work decides.** Escalation is for the exceptions listed below, not for reassurance. If a decision is reversible and inside the standards, make it and write it down.

## The table

| Decision | Decided by | Consulted | Written down where |
|---|---|---|---|
| Which three jobs a programme prioritises | Programme lead, with the client's business owner | Learning designer | The project brain's brief |
| Session design inside the standard | Learning designer | Programme lead | The project brain's design notes |
| Changing a programme's measure after it is agreed | Delivery director | Account director, client sponsor | Project decisions log |
| **Running sessions without a captured baseline** | **Delivery director only** | Leadership team | Project decisions log, always |
| Scope change inside an agreed phase | Programme lead | Account director | Project decisions log |
| Anything that changes the contracted scope or dates | Account director | Delivery director | Project decisions log and the client |
| Taking on work that fails the ICP | Leadership team | — | This brain |
| Exception to a non-goal in the strategy | Leadership team | — | This brain, with the reason |
| Changing brand guidelines | Marketing lead | Leadership team | The marketing brain |
| Changing the delivery model or the learning design standard | Delivery director | Leadership team | This brain |

## The three that are never delegated

1. **Taking work with no measure.** [[3-Acme Corp/01_Strategy/strategy-on-a-page|Strategy on a page]] calls this a non-goal, and it is the one exception that has never been granted.
2. **Publishing a client outcome we did not measure.** No role holds this right, at any level.
3. **Naming a client publicly.** Client sponsor first, in writing, then the account director. There is no shortcut and no implied consent from a good relationship.

## How a decision gets recorded

Every decision that changes what somebody else will do gets one entry: what was decided, by whom, when, what it means, and what it closes off. On a programme that goes in the project's decisions log — see [[5-Parts Unlimited Project/06_Log/decisions|the Parts Unlimited decisions log]] for a live one. If it is general, it goes here or into the note it affects.

The value is not the audit trail. It is that in month four, nobody reopens the thing that was settled in month one.
