# Market view — AI skills in large organisations

**What this is:** the pattern we see across our own engagements and buyer conversations. It is our observation, gathered from the organisations we work with, and it is written down so we stop rediscovering it. It is **not** published research and must never be presented as an industry benchmark — see [[3-Acme Corp/02_Proposition/differentiators|what we do not claim]].

## The shape of the market right now

**Licences are ahead of usage, everywhere.** Every organisation we have worked with in the last eighteen months bought access before it had any view of which jobs would change. Usage then settles at a low plateau within about a quarter and stays there until somebody intervenes deliberately.

**The plateau is not about scepticism.** The people who are not using it are not opposed to it. They tried it twice on something it was bad at, concluded it was overrated, and went back to their week. Nothing since has given them a reason to try a third time.

**Head office adopts, the front line does not.** Adoption concentrates where the work is already text-shaped and self-directed. It stalls where the work is interrupted, timed and customer-facing — which is usually where the money is made.

**Security is the pacing item, not the blocker.** In our experience the security team almost never says no outright. They say "not with that data", and the programme loses weeks because nobody asked them in month one.

## What buyers ask for versus what they need

| What they ask for | What the engagement turns out to need |
|---|---|
| Awareness training for everyone | Deep work with the three roles that touch the number |
| A tool comparison | A decision about which jobs are worth changing |
| Prompt libraries | A place for their own knowledge to live, so answers can be checked |
| A completion metric | A business number with a baseline |

The gap between those two columns is the whole of our proposition.

## What this means for us

- Lead with **the job**, never with the technology. The buyer's list of twenty jobs is the opening move.
- Get the **security conversation into stage 2** of [[3-Acme Corp/03_Sales/sales-process|the sales process]]. It is a design input.
- Target the **front line**, not head office. It is harder, it is where the plateau is, and it is where a measurable number lives.
- Expect the "**we can do this ourselves**" objection every time, and answer it with the year in production rather than with capability.

## What would change our mind

If a client's adoption plateau lifted on its own, without a deliberate intervention, our core assumption would be wrong. We have not seen it. We are watching for it.
