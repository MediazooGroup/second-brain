# Client insight themes

Six things buyers keep saying, in their words, and what each one actually means. Gathered from first meetings and win/loss reviews. Roles only, no names — see [[3-Acme Corp/06_Governance/ways-of-working|ways of working]].

## 1. "We've done the training. It didn't stick."

Said in some form in almost every first meeting. Underneath it is not a complaint about the training. It is that nobody changed what happened in the four weeks afterwards. This is the sentence [[3-Acme Corp/01_Strategy/mission|the mission]] is written to answer, and hearing it is a strong buying signal.

## 2. "We can do this ourselves."

The most common objection, and usually true in principle. It is rarely about capability — it is about not wanting to be dependent on a supplier. Answering it with credentials makes it worse. Answering it with "you could; we'd be handing you the year we already spent finding where this breaks", and then offering the method openly, defuses it. See [[3-Acme Corp/02_Proposition/differentiators|differentiators]].

## 3. "Our people are too busy."

Never means what it says. It means *the last thing we asked them to do wasn't worth the time*. The counter is the shape of the ask: two half-days, on their own work, producing something they keep. Arguing about capacity loses.

## 4. "We just need awareness first."

The most expensive sentence in the market. It sounds like a small, safe start and it produces exactly the plateau described in [[3-Acme Corp/07_Research/market-view-ai-skills|the market view]]. When we hear it, the move is to agree with the instinct and change the unit: not everyone lightly, but three roles properly.

## 5. "IT will never allow it."

Usually said by the business, rarely by IT. When we get the security team into the room early, the answer is almost always a constraint rather than a refusal — *not with that data*, *not in that tool*, *not without a log*. A constraint is designable. A rumour is not.

## 6. "Show me it worked somewhere like us."

The one we are worst at answering. We have one published outcome story. This theme is the direct voice-of-customer evidence behind [[3-Acme Corp/01_Strategy/okrs-2026|KR1.3]] and it is why the case-study ask now happens in the same conversation as the outcome report.

## The pattern underneath all six

Buyers are not sceptical about the technology and they are not short of ideas. They are short of **proof that anything changes afterwards**. Every one of the six themes is a version of that. Which is why O3 — prove the outcome — is the objective that unlocks the other two, however unglamorous it looks on a slide.
