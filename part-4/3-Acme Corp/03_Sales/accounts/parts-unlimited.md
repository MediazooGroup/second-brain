# Account: Parts Unlimited

Our clearest example of [[3-Acme Corp/03_Sales/ideal-client-profile|the ideal client profile]], and the account currently carrying three key results at once.

## The company

Parts Unlimited distributes automotive parts to independent garages, fleet operators and trade counters. **4,200 people, 60 branches and two distribution centres across the UK and Ireland.** The commercial engine is the aftermarket side: a garage calls or emails, a branch quotes, and whoever chases the quote fastest usually wins the job.

## Why we are in the building

They rolled AI licences out to the whole organisation in January 2026 with a launch email and a lunchtime webinar. By April, monthly active use had settled at **14%**, and the people using it were mostly in head office. The branches — where the money is made — had not touched it.

Their aftermarket operations director did not want another awareness campaign. He wanted the quote follow-up time down, and he already reported that number weekly by branch. That combination is exactly what [[3-Acme Corp/03_Sales/ideal-client-profile|the ICP]] is written to find.

## What we sold

An [[3-Acme Corp/02_Proposition/offers/ai-adoption-programme|AI adoption programme]], phase one, in aftermarket operations. Three cohorts, 48 delegates, running through September 2026 to a hard close on **30 September**. Full delivery detail lives in its own brain: [[5-Parts Unlimited Project/INDEX|Parts Unlimited project brain]].

Two things about this deal are worth copying:

1. **The security conversation happened in stage 2.** Their IT security lead was in the second meeting, which is where the "customer data does not leave our environment" constraint came from. It shaped the design instead of derailing it.
2. **The baseline was contracted as a named deliverable with a date**, not folded into discovery. This is the first engagement built that way, and it is the test case for the [[3-Acme Corp/01_Strategy/okrs-2026|KR3.2]] fix.

## The twelve-month plan

- **Now to 30 September** — deliver phase one and land the measure. Nothing else is sold into this account until that number exists.
- **October** — outcome report to their board, and the ask for the case study. Their annual operations conference on 8 October is the reason the 30 September date is immovable.
- **Q4** — phase two: the distribution centres, as a second AI adoption programme. Different jobs, same method.
- **Q1 2027** — [[3-Acme Corp/02_Proposition/offers/leadership-development|leadership development]] for the branch manager population, if phase one's managers hold the habit at day 30.

## Risks to the account

- The programme closes on time but the measure does not, because the second measurement window collides with their September trading peak.
- The relationship currently sits with two people. If either moves, we are exposed.
- Phase two is being discussed before phase one has a number. Hold the line — see [[3-Acme Corp/03_Sales/commercial-model|the commercial model]].
