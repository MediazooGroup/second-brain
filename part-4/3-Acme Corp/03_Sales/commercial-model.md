# Commercial model

How a deal is *shaped*. **This note carries no rates, fees or figures by design** — numbers live in the finance system, are quoted per engagement, and are never written into the brain. See [[3-Acme Corp/06_Governance/ways-of-working|ways of working]] for why.

## The unit we sell

**A programme, not a day.** The client buys an outcome with a measure attached, delivered over a defined number of weeks to a defined number of cohorts. We do not sell facilitator days, and we do not sell seats.

This matters more than it sounds. A day rate makes the client optimise for fewer days, which is exactly the wrong thing to optimise. A programme price makes both sides optimise for the outcome.

## What a deal is built from

| Component | Driver | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery and prioritisation | Fixed | One half-day, plus the security conversation and baseline capture |
| Design | Number of distinct jobs being changed | Three is the standard; more needs a conversation |
| Delivery | Number of cohorts × sessions per cohort | Three cohorts is the ceiling before a design review |
| Embedding | Fixed 30-day window | Manager guide, check-ins, the second measurement |
| Proof | Fixed | The outcome report, written with the client |

## The shape that works

- **Two phases, not one.** Phase one proves it in a single department. Phase two is the next department, priced separately once phase one has a measured outcome. Clients say yes to phase one much faster when phase two is not in the same signature.
- **Milestone-based invoicing**, tied to the phase gates in [[3-Acme Corp/05_Product/delivery-model|the delivery model]] — never to elapsed time.
- **The baseline is a paid, named deliverable.** When it was folded into "discovery" for free it kept getting dropped, and that is most of why [[3-Acme Corp/01_Strategy/okrs-2026|KR3.2]] is behind.

## What we will not agree to

- Payment terms tied to satisfaction scores.
- Unlimited cohorts inside a fixed price.
- A discount in exchange for a case study. The case study is earned by the outcome or it is not worth having.
- Anything that requires us to procure licences or tooling on the client's behalf.
