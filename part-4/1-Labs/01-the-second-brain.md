# The second brain

![[1-Labs/images/01-the-second-brain.png]]

You consume more than you ever have. Newsletters, calls, decks, threads, documents. And almost none of it compounds. Next month you start from scratch again, on a subject you already spent a week understanding.

That is the problem a second brain solves. It is a folder of your own material, in plain files, that you and an AI can both read. Your thinking accumulates instead of washing past.

Three things made it worth doing now, when for twenty years it was a filing cabinet you had to maintain:

1. **The files are plain.** No app owns them. They are yours in ten years.
2. **An assistant can read the whole folder at once.** You ask in ordinary English.
3. **Every answer names the file it came from.** You can check it.

That last one is the whole game. An answer you can check is worth ten you have to trust.

If you missed it, part one is here: `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6zSG6SWA-Js`

**Today we take exactly this idea and make it bigger than one person.** Same folder, same plain files, same checkable answers — pointed at an organisation, a department and a project.
