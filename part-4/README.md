## The four sizes

A second brain is one habit. Where you point it is the only thing that changes.

**Personal.** What you have been building for three weeks. Your notes, your
questions, your folder.

**Project.** One deliverable, one deadline, one client. The shortest life and
the sharpest payoff. `5-Parts Unlimited Project/` is one of these: a training
programme being delivered to a client with six weeks left on the clock. Brief,
deliverables, delegates, the knowledge and skills and behaviours being taught,
milestones, decisions, risks, status.

**Departmental.** One function, running on process rather than on the people
who happen to work there this year. `4-Acme Marketing/` is one of these:
strategy, audience and personas, proposition, content, the campaigns that
actually work, governance, brand guidelines.

**Organisational.** The whole company's shape. `3-Acme Corp/` is one of these:
strategy and OKRs, proposition, sales, marketing, product, governance,
research, brand.

## The thing worth noticing

Open `4-Acme Marketing/` and look for where it cites the organisation's OKRs.
Then open `5-Parts Unlimited Project/` and look for where it reaches back to
the company's brand kit.

The brains link **upward and downward**. That is not decoration. It is why you
can ask a question of one and get an answer that reasons across all three, and
why a department stops quietly drifting away from what the company said it was
doing.

## Try it yourself

Point an assistant at one of these folders and ask it things. Each lab page in
`1-Labs/` ends with the questions we asked live, so you have a ready-made set
to start from.

Then do the honest version: point it at a folder of your own.

Start with the smallest one. A project with a deadline is the easiest place to
begin, because you find out inside a week whether it is helping.
