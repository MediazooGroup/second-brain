# Inbox — August live session, poll results and questions

Dropped in raw on 17 August. **Not filed yet.** Triage on Monday: some of this belongs in the personas, some in the campaign, and some of it is noise.

## The polls

192 attendees, 141 answered at least one poll.

**"Has your organisation rolled out AI licences?"**
Yes, everyone — 61%. Yes, some teams — 27%. Not yet — 12%.

**"Roughly what share of people with a licence use it in a normal week?"**
Under a quarter — 58%. A quarter to a half — 29%. Over half — 13%.

**"Where is adoption strongest?"**
Head office / central functions — 71%. Evenly spread — 18%. Front line / operations — 11%.

**"What stopped the last change from sticking?"**
Nothing changed in the four weeks after — 44%. No time — 31%. Wrong people trained — 15%. Tooling — 10%.

## The questions people asked

The best twenty minutes, as usual. Roughly grouped:

- Several versions of "how do you get the front line to engage when they're on the counter all day?" — by far the most asked.
- "How do you measure this without adding another reporting burden?"
- "What do we do about customer data? Our security team won't allow it in the tool."
- "How many people do you actually need to train before it changes anything?"
- "Do you have anything from a distributed business rather than an office one?"
- Two people asked, separately, whether the folder structure works for a department that isn't marketing.

## What I think this means — to be tested on Monday

- The 71% / 11% split is the **plateau finding from [[3-Acme Corp/07_Research/market-view-ai-skills|the market view]] confirmed by a live audience**, and it is a much better hook for [[4-Acme Marketing/05_Campaigns/campaign-ai-ready-teams|the autumn campaign]] than the wording we have. Worth changing the October push.
- "Nothing changed in the four weeks after" at 44% is [[3-Acme Corp/07_Research/client-insight-themes|theme 1]], stated by the audience unprompted. That is a line for the campaign page.
- The security question came up again. It keeps coming up. It may deserve its own piece rather than a paragraph in the readiness guide.
- "Anything from a distributed business rather than an office one?" is [[4-Acme Marketing/03_Proposition/proposition-and-proof|the front-line proof gap]], asked out loud by a prospect. Nothing to do about it until 30 September.

2. Decide whether the security question becomes its own asset.
3. Ask the two people who asked about non-marketing folder structures whether they want a conversation.
