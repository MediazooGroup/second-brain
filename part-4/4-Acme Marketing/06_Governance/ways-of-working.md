# Ways of working — the marketing department

How this department runs, written down so it depends on process rather than on any one person. If somebody joins on Monday, this page and [[4-Acme Marketing/INDEX|the index]] are the two things they read first.

## Who does what

**Roles, not people** — the role holds the work, and it moves with the role.

| Role | Owns |
|---|---|
| Marketing lead | The strategy, the play mix, the account play with sales, brand |
| Content lead | The content engine, plays 1, 2 and 4, the review gate |
| Campaign coordinator | Campaign sequencing, the site, email, the calendar |
| Everyone | Capturing what they hear into the inbox, same week |

Nothing in this department is owned by "marketing". Every play, campaign and standing job has one named role against it.

## The rhythms

- **Monday, 30 minutes** — inbox triage. Everything captured last week is filed, drafted or deleted. The inbox is empty by Monday lunchtime or somebody explains why.
- **Weekly** — the content rhythm in [[4-Acme Marketing/04_Content/content-engine|the content engine]].
- **Fortnightly, with sales** — the account play. Which two accounts, what we are saying, who sends it.
- **Monthly** — play review. What each play produced, what we are stopping.
- **Quarterly** — strategy check against the company OKRs, and the numbers in [[4-Acme Marketing/01_Strategy/marketing-strategy|the strategy]] updated in place.

## How this brain is kept

- **Front matter on every note:** title, type, tags, captured, source, status. Add `reviewed` when it is checked.
- **One idea per note.** If it needs two headings that could each be a page, it is two pages.
- **Link, never copy.** A fact written down twice disagrees with itself within a quarter. This applies especially upward: the company brain owns the mission, the OKRs and the voice, and we link to them rather than restating them.
- **Everything is on [[4-Acme Marketing/INDEX|the index]].** If it is not, it does not exist.
- **`00_Inbox` is temporary.** Nothing lives there longer than a week.
- **Status is honest.** `locked` means do not change without a decision. `draft` means do not quote it externally.

## What lives here, and what does not

**Here:** personas, plays, campaigns, content standards, brand guidelines, our own research.

**Not here:** the mission, the OKRs, the offers and the sales process — those are the company's, in [[3-Acme Corp/INDEX|the Acme Corp brain]]. Client-confidential material — that stays in the account and project brains. Individual performance, of people or of posts.

## The handover test

The test we hold this department to: **if the marketing lead is away for a month, does anything stop?** The plays have owners, the cadences are written down, the standards are on a page, and the review gate has three questions anyone can ask.

That is the whole point of a departmental brain. Not documentation for its own sake — a department that runs on process rather than on somebody's memory.
