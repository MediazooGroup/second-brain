# Proposition and proof

What we are allowed to claim, and what we can point at when someone asks us to back it up. The proposition itself is the company's ([[3-Acme Corp/02_Proposition/offer-catalogue|offer catalogue]]); this note is marketing's ledger of the evidence behind it.

## The proposition, as marketing says it

> We turn new ways of working into everyday habits. The measure is agreed on day one, the work is built with your people on their own files, and you can run the next cohort without us.

## The proof ledger

Every claim, and the strongest thing we can put in front of a sceptic. **A claim with an empty proof column does not go into published material.**

| Claim | Proof we hold | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| Programmes produce a measured outcome | One published outcome story, with the number and the method | **Thin.** One is not a pattern, and we say "one" |
| In production for over a year | Six live engagements on the repeatable design; the method's own version history | Good |
| Delegates use it after we leave | Day-30 applied-it-at-work reporting, currently 66% across programmes | Good, and honest about the number |
| We hand over the method | The handover material itself, publishable in full | Strong — we can just show it |
| We prioritise before designing | Described, never demonstrated publicly, by design | Adequate. Do not over-claim |
| Front-line adoption, not just head office | One programme in delivery aimed squarely at the front line | **Thin.** Pending |

## The two gaps, named

**Only one published outcome story.** It is the single biggest constraint on this department and on the company's flagship objective ([[3-Acme Corp/01_Strategy/okrs-2026|KR1.3]]). Everything in [[4-Acme Marketing/01_Strategy/marketing-strategy|the strategy]] is arranged around closing it.

**No front-line proof yet.** Every story we hold is from a head-office population, which is exactly the objection [[4-Acme Marketing/02_Audience/persona-operations-director|the operations director]] raises. The Parts Unlimited programme is the first front-line engagement and closes on 30 September.

## The rules on proof

1. **We only publish numbers we measured ourselves.** No industry benchmarks, no third-party averages, no "up to".
2. **One story means we say one.** Not "our clients", not "organisations like yours".
3. **The honest bit stays in.** Every outcome story says what did not work. It is the reason the rest is believed.
4. **The client approves the words**, not just the fact. Sponsor first, in writing.
5. **No client is named without written permission**, and that permission is per use, not forever.
