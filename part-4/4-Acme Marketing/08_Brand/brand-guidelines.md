# Brand guidelines

**The authority.** The organisational brain carries a summary at [[3-Acme Corp/08_Brand/brand-kit|brand kit]]; if the two disagree, this note wins and that one gets corrected. Marketing owns changes to this page — see [[3-Acme Corp/06_Governance/decision-rights|decision rights]].

## Colour

| Name | Hex | Role | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Acme Deep | `#12233A` | Primary | Text, headings, the mark. Carries every piece |
| Acme Paper | `#F7F6F2` | Background | Never pure white. Never a gradient |
| Acme Spark | `#F2A104` | Accent | **One highlight per page or slide.** Never for body text |
| Acme Sage | `#4C8577` | Secondary | Diagrams, panels, supporting elements |
| Acme Slate | `#5A6472` | Tertiary | Captions, secondary labels, table rules |

**The two-colour rule:** Deep and Paper carry any single piece. Spark and Sage are each allowed one job in a frame, and never both at full strength.

**Accessibility:** Deep on Paper is the default text pairing. Spark is never used as text on Paper — it fails contrast and it looks cheap.

## Type

- **Headings:** Inter, semibold, tracking −1%.
- **Body:** Inter regular, 16px minimum on screen, 1.6 line height.
- **Long-form documents:** Source Serif at body size, Inter for headings.
- **Never:** more than two weights in one document. All-caps beyond a two-word label. Justified text. Italic for emphasis — use bold.

## The mark

"Acme" in Acme Deep, with a single Spark underscore beneath the wordmark.

**Clear space:** one cap-height on all four sides. **Minimum size:** 24px high on screen, 12mm in print.

**Never:** on a photograph. Recoloured. Outlined. Rotated. Stretched. Inside a shape or a badge. Beside another logo without the co-branding lockup below. Animated.

## The motif

A small square, repeated and growing — one, then four, then nine. It carries [[3-Acme Corp/01_Strategy/mission|the mission]]: something small, repeated, that becomes the way things are done.

It is a motif, not a logo. It is used at low contrast, as a background or a divider, in Sage or Slate. **It never appears in the same frame as the wordmark**, and it is never used as an icon or an avatar.

## Imagery

- **Real environments, real people doing their actual job.** A branch counter, a depot, a desk with paperwork on it.
- **No stock training rooms.** No smiling groups around a laptop. No abstract glowing brains, circuits, or robots — ever, and especially not on AI material.
- Photography is warm, uncluttered and slightly under-saturated. It sits on Paper, not full-bleed.
- Delegate photography needs written permission, per client, per use.

## Co-branding on client work

Delegate-facing material for a client programme is **the client's brand first**. Acme appears once, small, in the footer, in Deep or reversed to white. We do not put our motif on a client's handbook and we do not impose our palette on their material.

This is a live decision on every programme; the current worked example is [[5-Parts Unlimited Project/03_Deliverables/deliverables|the Parts Unlimited deliverables]], where the delegate handbook is client-branded and the manager's guide is Acme-branded.

## The things we never do

1. Put the wordmark on a photograph.
2. Use Spark as body text.
3. Use the motif and the wordmark in the same frame.
4. Use stock imagery of training rooms, or any AI cliché.
5. Publish a number we did not measure ourselves ([[3-Acme Corp/05_Product/quality-bar|the quality bar]]).
6. Put our branding on something a client's comms team will send internally.
