# Tone of voice

The company voice is set upstairs in [[3-Acme Corp/08_Brand/voice-and-tone|voice and tone]] and does not change. This note is the channel-by-channel detail, plus the rules that only marketing needs.

## The voice, restated in one line

Plain, specific, direct, honest about limits, never hyped.

## Channel by channel

| Channel | Length | Opening move | Never |
|---|---|---|---|
| **LinkedIn post** | 120–250 words | A specific observation from real work | A hook question. "Let that sink in." Lists of five things nobody said |
| **Long-form page** | 800–1,500 words | The problem in the buyer's words | A definition of AI. A history of the technology |
| **Email** | Under 150 words | One idea, one link, no preamble | "Hope this finds you well." Three CTAs |
| **Outcome story** | One page | The number and the constraint | Adjectives. Our name in the first paragraph |
| **Live session** | — | The failure mode, first | Slides that read themselves aloud |
| **Account note** | Under 400 words | Something true about *their* organisation | Anything that could be sent to a different company |

## Marketing's three extra rules

1. **No claim without a source.** If a line has a number in it, the draft links the note it came from and the reviewer checks it. This is not bureaucracy: it is the only reason our claims are believable when everyone else's are not.
2. **Write to one persona per piece.** If you cannot say which of the [[4-Acme Marketing/02_Audience/ideal-customer-personas|three personas]] would read past the first line, the piece is written to nobody.
3. **Publish the method, hold the framework.** Everything about how the work is done is fair game. The prioritisation framework is not.

## Words

**We say:** job, habit, week, cohort, delegate, measure, baseline, stick, prove, plateau, front line.

**We do not say:** solution, journey, leverage, upskill, best-in-class, cutting-edge, seamless, empower, unlock, supercharge, revolutionise.

**Careful with "AI":** say what it does in the sentence — drafts the follow-up, summarises the call — rather than leaning on the two letters. In material for the operations director, the letters should barely appear.

**Careful with learning language:** "delegate", "cohort" and "capability" are correct with the L&D director and read as head office to the operations director. Swap to "the eighteen people", "the group", "what they can do".

## The test

Read it aloud. If you would not say it to a branch manager standing at a trade counter with three minutes to spare, rewrite it.
