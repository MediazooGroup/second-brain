# Campaign — AI-ready teams

Autumn 2026. The first campaign we have written primarily for [[4-Acme Marketing/02_Audience/persona-operations-director|the operations director]] rather than to the L&D director. In flight now; runs to 30 November.

## The insight it is built on

Organisations bought AI licences in January, watched usage settle at a low plateau by April, and are now heading into planning season needing to explain that spend. The buyer feeling it most is the one whose people are not using the licences and whose number has not moved.

That plateau is documented in [[3-Acme Corp/07_Research/market-view-ai-skills|the market view]] and it is the campaign's entire hook. We are not selling AI training. We are naming a thing they are already living with.

## The proposition, in one line

> You bought the licences. Your front line didn't. Here's what to do about it before planning season closes.

## What is in it

| Asset | For | Where |
|---|---|---|
| "The adoption plateau" — a short, ungated diagnostic page | Operations director | Site, no form |
| The readiness guide, refreshed for autumn | L&D director | Site, no gate |
| Four ways an AI programme stalls | Both | Long-form post + email |
| The delegate film from the first outcome story | Operations director | Site, LinkedIn, and offered for internal use |
| A live session: "which three jobs are worth changing first" | L&D director | Play 2, October slot |
| Ten personalised account notes | Named accounts | Play 3, run with sales |

## Sequence

1. **Late August** — the diagnostic page and the stall post go live. No email push yet.
2. **September** — the live session, and the account notes go out in pairs.
3. **Early October** — planning-season push, when budgets are actually being written.
4. **Mid October** — the second outcome story lands, if the Parts Unlimited measure holds. This is the campaign's payload and everything before it is set-up.
5. **November** — the second delegate film, and a review.

## The dependency, named honestly

Step 4 depends on a client programme closing on 30 September with a measured result, and on a client comms lead approving a story about it. Neither is ours. If the measure slips, the campaign runs to November on set-up material alone and lands considerably softer.

**Mitigation:** the campaign is designed so steps 1 to 3 stand on their own, and the October push does not depend on the story existing. We have committed to no dates publicly.

## How we will know it worked

- Conversations started by an operations director, directly or via their L&D director — currently near zero, which is the point.
- Requests for the diagnostic from named accounts.
- Not: page views, downloads, or open rates. None of those move anything upstairs.
