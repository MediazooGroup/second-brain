# Positioning and messaging

The words. What we claim, in what order, to whom.

## The positioning statement

> For large, distributed organisations that have decided to work differently and cannot get the new way to stick, **Acme Corp** turns new ways of working into everyday habits — with the measure agreed on day one, and a year of production experience behind the method.

Unlike training suppliers, we do not sell a curriculum. Unlike consultancies, we do not leave a recommendation. We leave a habit and a number.

## The message hierarchy

Used in this order, always. Most of our weaker material inverts it and leads with capability.

1. **The problem, in their words.** "You've done the training. It didn't stick."
2. **The reframe.** The unit that matters is behaviour four weeks later, not attendance.
3. **The proof.** A measured outcome from a real client.
4. **The method.** Prioritise, build with the delegates, measure against a baseline.
5. **The ask.** A conversation about which three jobs are worth changing first.

## The five claims, and how we are allowed to say them

Sourced from [[3-Acme Corp/02_Proposition/differentiators|the differentiators]] and constrained by [[3-Acme Corp/05_Product/quality-bar|the quality bar]].

| Claim | The copy line | The rule |
|---|---|---|
| In production over a year | "We've been doing this in production for over a year." | Never inflate to "years". |
| We prioritise first | "Twenty ideas in, three worth doing out." | Describe what it does, never publish the framework. |
| Measure on day one | "The number is agreed before we design a session." | Only quote numbers we measured ourselves. |
| Built with delegates | "Their own work, from the first hour." | No stock imagery of a training room. |
| We hand over the method | "You can run the next cohort without us." | Say it plainly; do not hedge it. |

## The one thing we hold back

**The prioritisation framework itself.** We publish that we prioritise, what it produces and why it matters. We do not publish the matrix. It is the thing being bought, and giving it away turns a differentiator into a blog post.

Everything else — the delivery model, the KSB standard, the folder structures, the failure modes — is published freely. That asymmetry is deliberate and it is the whole of move 2 in [[4-Acme Marketing/01_Strategy/marketing-strategy|the strategy]].

## Words

Follow [[3-Acme Corp/08_Brand/voice-and-tone|the company voice]] without exception. Marketing's extra rule: **no claim without a source.** If a line has a number in it, the note it came from is linked in the draft, and the reviewer checks it.

## Messaging by persona

- **L&D director** — lead with "it didn't stick" and the day-30 measure.
- **Operations director** — lead with the number. Skip the learning language entirely.
- **Internal comms lead** — lead with the delegate's own story, and how it gets told.
