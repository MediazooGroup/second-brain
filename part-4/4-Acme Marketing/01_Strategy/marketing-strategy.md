# Marketing strategy 2026

What this department is trying to do this year, in one page, so every play, campaign and piece of content can be checked against it.

## The job

Make the right buyer arrive already believing two things: **that Acme makes change stick, and that we have been doing it in production for over a year.** Everything else is tactics.

## What we are accountable for

Marketing owns one company key result outright and materially affects two more. The objectives live upstairs in [[3-Acme Corp/01_Strategy/okrs-2026|Acme Corp OKRs 2026]]; this is our part of them.

| Company key result | Our part | At 5 August |
|---|---|---|
| **KR1.3 — three published client outcome stories** | **Owned outright** | 1 of 3. The department's biggest job |
| KR1.1 — AI adoption at 40% of delivered revenue | Demand into the flagship offer | Pipeline healthy, conversion held back by thin proof |
| KR2.2 — top-10 accounts with a twelve-month plan | The account-based play, run with sales | 8 of 10 |

## The strategy in three moves

**1. Proof before volume.** We are not short of attention; we are short of evidence. A single published outcome story with a real number in it out-converts a quarter of thought leadership. Everything we do this year is arranged around getting the second and third stories out.

**2. Teach the method, openly.** Our buyers ask "can we do this ourselves?" ([[3-Acme Corp/07_Research/client-insight-themes|theme 2]]). Answering by hiding the method makes it worse. We publish how the work is done, in public, and let the year of production experience be the thing that is hard to copy. We hold back exactly one thing: the prioritisation framework itself.

**3. Two audiences, one story.** The L&D director buys the programme; the operations director owns the number that justifies it. We have historically written only to the first. Half of this year's content is written to the second — see [[4-Acme Marketing/02_Audience/persona-operations-director|the operations director persona]].

## Where the effort goes

Roughly, across the year: **40% into the outcome-story play**, 25% into the live teaching series, 20% into the account-based play with sales, 15% into everything else. If a request does not fit one of those, it needs a reason.

The plays themselves, with owners and cadence: [[4-Acme Marketing/05_Campaigns/marketing-plays|marketing plays]].

## The dependency we cannot design around

We cannot manufacture an outcome story. It needs a measured outcome to exist, which is delivery's gate, not ours — and [[3-Acme Corp/01_Strategy/okrs-2026|KR3.2]] sits at 41%. The agreed rule since the half-year review is that **the case-study ask happens in the same conversation as the outcome report**, at programme close. Marketing is in that conversation, or it does not happen.

The next realistic story is the Parts Unlimited programme, closing 30 September. It is on our plan as a September dependency, not an October surprise.

## What we stopped doing this year

- Gated whitepapers with no teaching in them. They produced volume and no conversations.
- Sponsoring events where we could not get a speaking slot.
- Writing to "L&D and HR leaders" as one audience. They are not one audience.
