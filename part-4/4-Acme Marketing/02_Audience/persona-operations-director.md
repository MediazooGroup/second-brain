# Persona — the operations director

**The economic owner.** Owns the number the change is supposed to move. Historically ignored by our marketing, and the reason half of this year's content exists.

## Who they are

Runs a commercial operation across branches, depots, sites or service centres. Hundreds to a few thousand people underneath them. Reports weekly on a small number of things and is measured on those things without much sympathy.

They are not in the market for training. They are in the market for a number to move.

## What they are trying to do this quarter

- Move one metric they already report on, without adding headcount.
- Stop losing capacity to initiatives that arrive from head office.
- Get the front line to actually adopt something, for once, rather than nodding at it.

## What they say

> "Show me it worked somewhere like us."

> "I can't take eighteen branch managers off the floor for two days in September."

> "We bought the licences in January. Nobody's using them."

## What they actually fear

Disruption with nothing to show for it. They have been through initiatives that cost them a week of capacity and returned a set of slides. Being the director who let that happen again is worse, for them, than missing out on a new technology.

## What makes them trust us

- **Leading with their number, not with ours.** If the first sentence is about learning, we have lost them.
- Specificity: minutes per quote, days to respond, rework rate. Not "efficiency".
- Honesty about the cost in capacity. Two half-days, a week apart, and what happens between them. Stated up front, not softened.
- Somebody who has stood on a shop floor. They can hear it in about a minute.

## What loses them

- Learning language of any kind. "Delegate", "cohort", "capability" all read as head office.
- A benchmark from an industry report. They do not believe it and they will say so.
- Any answer to "how long will this take my people?" that is not a number.

## Where they are

Almost nowhere we control. They do not read L&D content, do not attend our webinars, and do not engage with cold outreach. They are reached in exactly two ways: **a peer in the same sector telling them something worked**, and **their own L&D director bringing them a proposal with a number already attached**.

Which is why the outcome story is the highest-leverage thing this department makes — it is the only asset that travels to this persona.

## What to send them

- One page. The number, the constraint, what it cost in capacity, what happened after 30 days.
- A short film of a delegate in their own environment saying what changed. Not a testimonial about us.
- Nothing gated, ever.
