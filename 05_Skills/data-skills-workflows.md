# The Data → Skills → Workflows ladder

The whole idea in three rungs. Each rung stands on the one below. This is why we
always say the second brain is step one, not the destination.

## The ladder

```
WORKFLOWS   chain skills together into a whole job, done end to end
   ▲
SKILLS      reusable instructions the AI runs against your data
   ▲
DATA        your second brain — your knowledge, in plain files you own
```

### 1. Data — your second brain

Your knowledge and thinking, captured in plain files you own and control. This
is what the four-week series builds. Without it, everything above is generic.

### 2. Skills — built on the data

Saved instructions the assistant runs against that data: "turn this mess into a
filed note", "draft a newsletter from my saved ideas", "run my weekly review".
Written once, reused forever. (See [[05_Skills/what-are-skills]].)

### 3. Workflows — skills chained together

Several skills run in sequence to finish a whole job with one nudge. Example:
*capture a source → file it → pull the reusable idea out of it → draft a post
from that idea → queue it for review.* Five skills, one workflow, one command.

## Why it matters for the viewer

Most people are told to "use AI" and handed a chat box with no context. That is
starting at the top of the ladder with nothing underneath. We start at the
bottom: **build the data first**, then skills, then workflows. That is the only
order that gives you answers grounded in your own work rather than the internet's
average.
