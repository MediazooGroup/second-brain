# Example skills you can build

Concrete skills, each one a recipe you write once and run forever, all pointed at
your own second brain. Pitched at our audience: people who work with ideas and
content.

## For the marketer / creator

- **The repurposer.** "Take one idea from my brain and give me a newsletter, a
  LinkedIn post, and three hooks from it." One idea in, a week of content out.
- **The draft-from-my-thinking skill.** "Draft a first pass on [topic] using only
  what I already believe, in my voice." No more blank page, no more generic AI
  waffle.
- **The swipe-matcher.** "Find the saved hook shape that best fits this idea and
  write the opening line."

## For everyone

- **The filer** (this is Part 3). "Take this messy source and turn it into a
  clean, properly filed note. Show me before you save."
- **The weekly review.** "Walk my inbox with me, suggest where each capture
  should go, and flag anything I've left raw too long."
- **The connector.** "What in my brain relates to [theme]? Show me links I might
  have missed." Surfaces thinking you forgot you had.
- **The catch-up.** "Summarise everything I've captured this month and tell me
  the one idea worth acting on."

## The pattern behind all of them

Every skill has the same shape:

> **Read** something from my brain → **do** a specific job → **show** me the
> result before anything is saved.

That last step is the trust rule: you always get the last word. Write the recipe
once; the assistant runs it on demand.
