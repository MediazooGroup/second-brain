# Persona — the creator

Building an audience from their own expertise: a newsletter, a channel, a
personal brand. Often a team of one. Motivated, but drowning in saved posts,
half-finished drafts and screenshots that never become anything.

## In their words

> "My notes app is a graveyard. I save everything and use none of it. I don't
> need more ideas, I need to turn the ones I have into things I actually
> publish."

## What they want

- To publish consistently without burning out or running dry.
- To sound like themselves, at scale, drawing on months of their own thinking.
- Proof they can do this without a full production team.

## What blocks them

- Capture with no processing: ideas pile up and rot.
- The blank page, every time, because past thinking is unfindable.

## How the series serves them

Capture fast, process into atoms, and let the AI help you create — that is the
whole loop. This persona feels the "consume vs compound" pain most sharply. See
[[03_Series/part-4-keep-it-alive]] for the habit that saves them.
