# Persona — the marketer

Turns ideas into content, campaigns and messaging. Reads constantly for the job.
Tired of starting every brief, post and deck from a blank page when they know
they have thought about this before, somewhere.

## In their words

> "I've written about this three times already. I just can't find any of it, so
> I write it again from scratch. Every single time."

## What they want

- To reuse their own best thinking instead of rewriting it.
- A faster path from "idea" to "published", without the quality dropping.
- Angles and hooks on tap, drawn from what they already believe.

## What blocks them

- Good ideas trapped inside finished pieces, impossible to find later.
- No system, so everything lives in their head or scattered across apps.

## How the series serves them

A second brain turns every idea into a reusable atom, and the AI on top turns
those atoms into drafts. This is the persona most of the live examples are
pitched at. See [[05_Skills/example-skills]] for the content skills they can
build.
