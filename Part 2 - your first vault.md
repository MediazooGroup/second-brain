# Part 2 - your first vault

**Wed 5 Aug, 1pm BST. One hour. This is the one this folder came from.**

## The promise

Markdown from zero, and your first real note, in files you own, shaped around
your own questions rather than someone else's system.

## What it covered

Six labs, done live, in this order. Every one of them is written out step by
step in [[README]].

1. **Install Obsidian.** Free, small, no account.
2. **Create your first vault.** A vault is a folder. That is the whole idea.
3. **Your first markdown note.** Five marks, and there is no sixth. See
   [[Markdown in five marks]].
4. **Link documents together.** Write a link in one direction, get it back in
   both. This is the move that turns files into a brain.
5. **Front matter, and what it is for.** The small block at the top that lets
   you find a note in two years. See [[Front matter]].
6. **Progressive disclosure and the root index.** Shape first, detail on
   request. That is what [[INDEX]] is doing.

Then a fully built second brain was opened at the end, to show where six labs
eventually take you. Same five marks. Just more of them.

## The note written live

The question on the day was: **what actually makes our content land?**

Both notes are in this folder, exactly as they were typed:
[[What actually makes our content land]] and [[Things that landed]].

## What it left you with

Your first note, and a home for it. And, if you want it,
