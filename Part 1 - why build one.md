# Part 1 - why build one

**Wed 29 Jul, 1pm BST. Thirty minutes.**

## The promise

You consume more than ever and almost none of it compounds. See what a second
brain is, and watch a real one work.

## What it covered

- **Information is more abundant than ever, and making it work is harder than
  ever.** More input has not made anyone think better. Mostly it has made
  people feel behind.
- **Most of us take in thousands of ideas a year and almost none of them
  compound.** That is the problem worth solving, and it is not a memory
  problem.
- **A second brain is not a folder system.** It is a compounding knowledge
  system. See [[What a second brain is]].
- **The hundred year story.** This idea did not arrive with an app. Commonplace
  books, Vannevar Bush's memex, Tiago Forte's second brain, and more recently
  the idea of a wiki an assistant keeps current for you. Four hundred years of
  people arriving at the same answer.
- **A first taste of what AI changes.** Not retrieval. Reasoning with you, over
  your own material.

## The bit that stuck

Your mind is for having ideas, not for holding them.

## What it left you with

Knowing what a second brain is, and why now is the moment it finally works.
The building starts in [[Part 2 - your first vault]].
