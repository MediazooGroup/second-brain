# Part 4 - keep it alive

**Wed 19 Aug, 1pm BST. One hour. The last one.**

## The promise

The loop that survives a busy week: capture, sweep, review. Then we stand back
and look at everything you built.

## The loop, at three speeds

- **Capture, constantly, with zero thought.** If capturing costs you a decision,
  you will stop doing it. This is the speed that has to be free.
- **Sweep, weekly, a few minutes.** The tidying, done for you while you watch,
  using what [[Part 3 - hand the tidying over]] set up.
- **Review, monthly, ten honest minutes.** The only part that is genuinely
  yours, because it is judgement. What matters now, what has gone cold, what
  you were wrong about.

## Why this session exists

A second brain that needs you to be organised is a second brain you will
abandon in November.

So this one is not about more features. It is a design for the weeks when you
have no time, which is most weeks. The first sweep gets run live, and a
keep-alive routine gets pinned to a real day in a real week.

## Then we look back

The last part of the last session is standing back and looking at the whole
thing you built across four Wednesdays. It is worth staying for.
