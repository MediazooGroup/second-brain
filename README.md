# The Second Brain Blueprint — the series brain

This is the second brain for the live series you are watching right now. Every
decision behind these four streams lives here, in plain files: what we believe,
who it is for, what we cover each week, how it looks and sounds, and where it
goes next.

We are teaching you to build a second brain. So it only felt honest to run this
whole series out of one. Ask it anything about the series and it answers from
these notes. Nothing is hidden off-stage.

## How it is laid out

- `00_Inbox/` — raw captures we haven't filed yet. The capture habit in action.
- `01_Strategy/` — the north star, the plan, and how we know it worked.
- `02_Audience/` — who this is for, and what they walk away with.
- `03_Series/` — the four weeks, one file each, and the six beats that run
  through all of them.
- `04_Brand/` — how it looks and how it sounds.
- `05_Skills/` — the payoff: what you can build *on top of* a second brain once
  it exists, and what that actually means.
- `06_Sources/` — the hundred-year story this idea sits inside.

## How to read a file

Every note opens with a small block at the top — the frontmatter: `title`,
`tags`, `captured`, `source`, `status`. That block is what lets us (and the AI)
find and connect notes without opening every one. Start with [[INDEX]] for the
whole map.

**North star (the sentence everything ladders back to):**

> We consume more than ever, but very little compounds. A second brain makes
> your knowledge and insights accumulate, and in the age of AI, that becomes
> your engine for better thinking.
