# What "skills on top" means

This is the part most people miss, and it is where a second brain stops being
notes and starts being leverage.

## The plain-English definition

> A **skill** is a saved instruction the AI follows against your second brain —
> a job you do often, written down once, so it can be run again and again.

Think of a skill like a recipe you hand the assistant. "Here is how I like a
messy source turned into a clean note." "Here is how I turn one idea into a
week's content." "Here is how I run my weekly review." You write the recipe once.
After that, you just say the word and it runs, using *your* knowledge in *your*
brain.

## Why the brain has to come first

A skill is only as good as what it can read. An AI with no access to your
thinking gives you generic answers — the same ones everyone else gets. An AI
pointed at your second brain gives you answers built from *your* material, in
*your* voice, about *your* work. **The brain is the fuel. The skills are the
engine.** No fuel, no drive.

## Why this is the real payoff of the series

Parts 1 to 4 get you a living second brain. That is the foundation. Skills are
what you build on the foundation:

- You stop doing the same repetitive thinking-work by hand.
- Each skill you write is reusable forever — it compounds, just like the notes.
- Your best judgement gets encoded once and applied consistently, at speed.

This is what "your engine for better thinking" in the north star actually means
in practice.
