# ICP — who this is for

ICP means ideal customer profile: the person we are making this for, so every
example, every word and every demo is pitched at them and not at "everyone".

## The one-line ICP

> People who work with ideas for a living, feel the overload, and sense their
> own thinking should be compounding but isn't.

## The three we picture

We write to three closely-related people (full notes in this folder):

- **The L&D lead** — owns capability in an organisation. Wants their people to
  get ahead of AI, not just tolerate it.
- **The marketer** — turns ideas into content and campaigns, and is tired of
  starting every piece from a blank page.
- **The creator** — building an audience from their own expertise, drowning in
  saved posts that never turn into anything.

They differ in title but share one job: **take what they know and what they
consume, and make it produce something.**

## What they have in common (the real target)

- They consume a lot — newsletters, podcasts, docs, threads — and almost none of
  it compounds.
- They are not developers. "Markdown" and "vault" sound technical and off-putting.
- They have tried a notes app before and abandoned it.
- They are curious about AI but have not felt a use that is genuinely theirs.

## How this shapes the series

- Every example is about **ideas and content that compound**, never abstract
  productivity.
- We start from absolute zero on anything technical (see Part 2).
- We meet the "I've abandoned notes apps before" objection head-on (see Part 4).
