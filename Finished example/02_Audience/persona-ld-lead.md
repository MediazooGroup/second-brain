# Persona — the L&D lead

Owns learning and capability inside an organisation. Under real pressure to help
the workforce get ahead of AI, not just cope with it. Smart, busy, accountable
to a leadership team.

## In their words

> "Everyone's told me to 'do something about AI'. I don't need another tool
> demo. I need a way to build real capability that sticks after the workshop
> ends."

## What they want

- People who can actually think and work better with AI, not just click buttons.
- Something that outlasts a one-off training day.
- A way to show leadership real, compounding progress.

## What blocks them

- Drowning in tool hype; short on grounded method.
- Worried anything "technical" will lose the non-technical half of their team.

## How the series serves them

The second brain is the concrete, personal habit underneath "AI capability".
Start one yourself, feel it compound, and you have the template for a whole
team. See [[Finished example/05_Skills/data-skills-workflows]] for where that leads.
