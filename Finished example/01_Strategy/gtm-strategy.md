# GTM strategy

GTM means go-to-market: how we get this in front of the right people and turn a
first watch into a lasting relationship. Ours is simple and honest — teach
generously in public, and make the next step easy for the few who want to go
further.

## The path, in plain terms

```
Watch the live series  →  keep the free course  →  (if you want) book a call
(free, public)            (free, yours forever)     (only if you want help)
```

1. **Attract — the live series.** Four live streams on the Mediazoo YouTube
   channel, simulcast to LinkedIn. Free, public, no gate. The job of the series
   is to be genuinely useful on its own, so people show up, come back, and bring
   someone.
2. **Capture — the sign-up.** A landing page collects an email from anyone who
   wants the join links and the free course. That is the only thing we ask for,
   and only from people who opt in.
3. **Nurture — the free course.** *The Second Brain Blueprint* is a free,
   keep-it-forever course that goes deeper than the streams can. It does the
   teaching at the viewer's own pace and builds trust over weeks, not minutes.
4. **Convert — book a call.** For the few who want hands-on help with their own
   material, one clear door: book a call. No pressure, no prices flashed on
   screen. The offer is help, not a hard sell.

## The principles under it

- **Teach, don't pitch.** Every stream gives something away that stands alone.
  The offer is only ever mentioned once, at the end, as a door not a wall.
- **Own the relationship.** The email list is ours and the viewer's; we are not
  renting attention from an algorithm forever.
- **Give the best thing away.** The free course is not a teaser. It is the real
  thing. Generosity is the whole strategy.
