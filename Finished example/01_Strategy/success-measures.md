# Success measures

How we judge whether the series worked. We hold ourselves to the outcome for the
viewer, not the vanity number for us.

## What we count

- **Quality of questions asked.** In the live chat and after. Better questions
  mean the idea landed and people are applying it to their own work.
- **Actions taken.** People who actually start a vault, write a first note, and
  come back the next week having done something.
- **Return rate across the four weeks.** Do the same people come back? A series
  that compounds should pull its own audience forward.
- **Sign-ups who open the free course.** Not just the list size — the share who
  actually start learning.

## What we deliberately don't chase

- **Raw view counts.** A big number from people who watch thirty seconds and
  leave teaches us nothing and helps no one.
- **Virality.** A spike we can't repeat. We would rather a smaller room that
  keeps showing up. (This is the north star applied to our own metrics:
  consistency compounds, spikes don't.)

## The honest test

> A month after Part 4, are people still using the thing we taught them? If yes,
> it worked, whatever the view count said.
