# Voice

How everything sounds, on screen and out loud.

## The shape of a line

- **Direct, confident, second person.** We speak to one person: "Your mind is
  for having ideas. Not for holding them."
- **Short declarative pairs.** The second sentence lands the punch, and it is the
  one phrase set in Signal Orange: "Access is not the gap. **Repeatable work
  is.**"
- **Plain business English.** No jargon. If a viewer would need to look a word
  up, cut it.

## Hard rules

- **No exclamation marks. No emoji. Ever.**
- **British English.**
- **No prices on any surface.** The offer is help, not a number on screen.
- **No unapproved client names.**
- Sentence case everywhere, full stops on headings.

## What we sound like

- **Generous.** We teach first and give the best thing away free.
- **Honest.** We show the messy middle, not just the polished result. Nothing
  off-stage.
- **Ownership-minded.** "Everything you build stays with you." "No lock-in."

## The test

Read it aloud. If it sounds like a brochure, rewrite it until it sounds like a
smart person explaining something to a friend. That is the voice.
