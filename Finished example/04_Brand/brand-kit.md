# Brand kit

Everything that makes the series look like itself, on one page. Two accent
colours on cool, dark neutrals. Confident, clean, a little cinematic.

## Colour

| Name | Hex | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| **Signal Orange** | `#FF5A1E` | The one operative phrase per screen, and every call to action. The accent, never the field. |
| **Pulse Aqua** | `#46D6E6` | Atmosphere and information: pathways, labels, stats, the sequence numbers. |
| **Ink** | `#0F1117` | The lead dark background (a blue-black). |
| **Mist** | `#EEF0F5` | The cool light background. **Never cream.** |
| Black | `#000000` | Full-bleed backing and co-brand. |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Cards and clean type on dark. |

**The one rule to remember:** one operative phrase in Signal Orange per section,
everything else neutral. Orange is the spark, aqua is the mood.

## Type

- **Quantico Bold** — headings and the wordmark. Uppercase for the mark.
- **Hanken Grotesk** — everything else (weights 300 to 700).
- **Case and punctuation:** sentence case everywhere, even headings. Full stops
  on headings. No exclamation marks. No emoji.

## The motif

- **The neuron** — organic dot-and-lobe clusters (small circles joined by thin
  lines). It is the signature device: it says "connected thinking" without a
  word. Used filled in Signal Orange, or as thin orange strokes.
- **Hero gradient** — black to teal (`#000506 → #034D57 → #44D3E3`). Behind
  heroes and key art.
- **Thin orange rules** — 2px lines to underline and divide.

## Imagery

Cinematic, cool teal-graded, low-key. A subject looking up and off-frame into
light, with faint orange ember particles. Never bright stock photography, never
warm daylight, never cream.

## The wordmark

**FINER / VISION** stacked, Quantico Bold, uppercase, tight leading, in Signal
Orange (or white / black where needed). No separate logo shape — the type is
the logo.
