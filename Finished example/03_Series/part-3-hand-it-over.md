# Part 3 — hand the tidying over

**Wed 12 Aug, 1pm BST. One hour.**

## The promise

The turn the whole series builds to: your hands come off the filing. We hand a
messy source to an assistant and watch it become a clean, properly filed note,
narrating every single move. Nothing happens off-stage.

## What it teaches

- **Beat 4 in full:** AI is not just for finding things. It reasons *with* your
  material — files it, connects it, updates several pages from one new source.
  That is what turns a pile of notes into a living wiki that keeps itself
  current.
- **Beat 3 in full:** the second brain as a system, not a folder — because now it
  maintains itself.

## The trust deal (say it in full, on screen)

The assistant: reads everything, **never edits your original**, proposes before
it acts, shows exactly what changed, and you get the last word every time. This
is the answer to "isn't AI going to mangle my notes?" — no, because you are
always the editor.

## What lands in the viewer's hands

The confidence to let an assistant do the tidying they always hated, without
losing control of a single word.
