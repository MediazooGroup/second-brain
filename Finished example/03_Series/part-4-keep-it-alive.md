# Part 4 — keep it alive

**Wed 19 Aug, 1pm BST. One hour. The payoff.**

## The promise

Every note system you have ever abandoned died the same way: it made staying
organised your job, forever. This final session hands you a design that survives
instead. Then we stand back and look at everything you built.

## What it teaches

- **Beat 6, the payoff:** the goal was never a tidy vault. It is better thinking
  and better decisions. The vault is just the engine.
- **The loop with three speeds:**
  - **Capture** — constantly, with zero thought.
  - **Sweep** — weekly, a few minutes, done for you by the assistant while you
    watch.
  - **Review** — monthly, ten honest minutes of the one thing only you can do:
    judgement.

## What lands in the viewer's hands

A keep-alive routine pinned to a real day of their real week, plus a look back at
the whole thing they built across four weeks. This is the answer to the "I've
abandoned notes apps before" objection.

## Where it points next

Once the loop is alive, the brain is a foundation you can build *skills* on top
of — the real leverage. See `05_Skills/`.
