# The thinkers we stand on

The people whose ideas the series draws on, boiled down to the one thing we use
from each. Named openly — we teach in the open.

## Vannevar Bush — the memex (1945)

Imagined a personal machine that stored your knowledge and let you link ideas by
association. The founding vision of a second brain. **What we use:** the idea
that linking your own notes, not just filing them, is where the value is.

## Tiago Forte — building a second brain

Popularised the modern method and the phrase itself: capture what resonates,
organise it so it is actionable, and let your notes serve your real projects.
**What we use:** the discipline that a note is only worth keeping if it will be
used — capture in service of doing, not hoarding.

## Andrej Karpathy — let the assistant own the wiki

Framed the next step: don't maintain the knowledge base by hand, let an AI
assistant keep it current. **What we use:** the whole of Part 3 — hand the
tidying over, keep a living wiki, and still get the last word. This is the shift
that makes a second brain finally survivable.

## The throughline

Across a hundred years, one idea keeps returning: your best thinking should
accumulate and connect, not scatter and vanish. Each generation got a better
tool for it. Ours is the assistant.
