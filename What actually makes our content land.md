# What actually makes our content land

We publish a lot. Some of it lands, most of it goes past people, and I am never
quite sure which it will be before I press publish.

**The pieces that land are the ones that say a true thing out loud.**

What I keep noticing:

- it is specific enough to be wrong
- it sounds like a person, not a department
- it turns up where the audience already is

The map of all this will live in a file called `INDEX.md`. We build that at the
end.
